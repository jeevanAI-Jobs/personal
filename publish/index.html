Questions

Frequently asked questions

When will Direct Publish be available?
Direct Publish is in active development. It will be available to Jeevan AI users on paid plans. Create a free account now and you will be notified as soon as it launches.
Do I need to use Direct Publish to benefit from Jeevan AI?
No. The full content draft is already available today on all paid plans. You export the draft and publish it yourself. Direct Publish is an additional step that removes the manual copy-paste, but the core fix is available right now.
Will Direct Publish modify my existing pages?
No. Direct Publish only creates new pages. It will not edit, overwrite, or modify any existing pages on your site. Each content draft becomes a new page at the suggested URL slug.
Can I preview the page before it goes live?
Yes. Direct Publish includes a preview step that shows you exactly how the page will look on your site before publishing. You can edit the draft inline at that point if needed.
