Ask Claude What Your Brand's AI Visibility Score Is

Sep 16, 2026 6 min read Jeevan AI Team

Checking a client's AI visibility score means opening the Jeevan AI dashboard, logging in, selecting the brand, and navigating to the scan you care about. If you manage five clients and want a quick status on each before a morning call, that is five separate context switches before 9 AM.

We shipped a fix for this. Jeevan AI now has a native Claude connector. Once connected, you can ask your own Claude about any brand on your account and get real data back, without opening the dashboard at all.

⚠️
Beta status: The Claude connector is currently in beta. It works on Claude Desktop and claude.ai. If you run into issues after connecting, reach out through the dashboard before forming conclusions about whether it is stable for your workflow.

What it actually does

The connector gives your Claude read-only access to your Jeevan AI account data. It cannot start scans, generate content, or spend any of your scan credits. It surfaces what already exists from scans you have run.

Specifically, you can ask it about:

📊

Visibility scores and trends

Latest score for any brand on your account, plus how that score has moved across recent scan runs.

🔍

Competitor citations

Which competitors are being named by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity instead of your brand, broken down by platform.

📋

Open content gaps

The current list of gap topics where AI systems favor a competitor, with buying factor context.

📰

Mentions and scan history

Recent social and news mentions with sentiment, plus a full list of past scans and their dates.

📚

Knowledge Base content

Search what you have already added to the brand's Knowledge Base, useful for avoiding duplicate content creation.

Who gets the most out of this

The connector is most immediately useful for agencies running Jeevan AI on behalf of multiple brands. If your day involves checking in on five or ten clients, the friction of opening a separate dashboard for each one is real. Being able to ask Claude mid-conversation, while you are already there for content work or reporting, cuts that friction significantly.

In-house teams and solo founders who use Claude regularly for writing and research also benefit. Your brand context is one question away instead of requiring a tab switch mid-flow.

If you primarily work in the dashboard and are not already living in Claude for other tasks, the connector is useful but less transformative. The dashboard still gives you the full chart view and historical graphs in one place.

How to connect

  1. Open the Jeevan AI dashboard. Go to Settings (the sidebar icon that looks like a gear or the Settings link in your account menu).
  2. Find Connect Claude. Look for the "Integrations" or "Connections" section. You will see a "Connect Claude" option. Click it.
  3. Approve access in Claude's consent screen. Claude will show a read-only permission request from Jeevan AI. Approve it. This is Claude's own login flow, not a copied API key.
  4. Start asking. Open Claude Desktop or claude.ai and ask about any brand on your account. No further setup needed.

Setup takes about two minutes. The one-time approval step goes through Claude's own consent screen, so Jeevan AI never handles your Claude credentials directly.

What it cannot do

The connector is read-only. It will not start a new scan, generate content suggestions, change any account settings, or do anything that costs scan credits. If you ask it to "run a scan" or "create a gap brief," it will tell you it cannot take that action and point you to the dashboard.

It also currently works only with Claude, not with other AI assistants. If that changes, we will update this.

Frequently asked questions

What can I ask Claude about my brand after connecting Jeevan AI?

You can ask about your latest AI visibility score, score trends across recent scans, which competitors are being cited instead of your brand, open content gaps, recent mentions with sentiment, and what is in your Knowledge Base. The connector covers all brands on your account.

Does the connector work with both Claude Desktop and claude.ai?

Yes. After the one-time connect and approve step, it works in both Claude Desktop (Mac and Windows) and claude.ai. You do not need to connect separately for each surface.

Can it start scans or change anything on my account?

No. The connector is strictly read-only. It surfaces data from scans you have already run and cannot take any action on your account, spend credits, or change settings.

Who is this most useful for?

Agencies managing multiple client brands get the most immediate value. Instead of switching to a separate dashboard tab for each check-in, they can ask directly inside Claude while already working there. Solo founders and in-house teams who use Claude daily for content work also benefit from having brand context one question away.
