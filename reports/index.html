What is AI visibility and why does it matter for Indian D2C brands?
AI visibility measures how often and how accurately AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode mention and recommend your brand when buyers ask category questions. For Indian D2C brands, AI visibility is critical because an increasing share of purchase research now starts with an AI query rather than a Google search. A brand invisible to AI is invisible to that growing buyer segment.
How does Jeevan AI measure AI visibility for brands?
Jeevan AI scores brands on six factors: Entity Clarity, Extractable Structure, Schema Markup, FAQ Coverage, Answer-Led Content, and Specificity and Evidence. Each factor scores 0 to 100. The overall score reflects how reliably AI engines can identify, verify, and cite the brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode.
Can a Shark Tank India brand improve its AI visibility score?
Yes. The Shark Tank India backstory is a powerful credibility signal that AI engines would cite frequently, but only if it is structured correctly on the brand's own site. Adding a dedicated brand story page with founder narrative, investment details, and FAQPage schema can improve AI visibility scores by 15 to 25 points within 6 to 8 weeks.
What content changes improve a brand's ChatGPT citation rate the fastest?
The three fastest-impact changes are: adding FAQPage schema to category and home pages, publishing a dedicated comparison page answering buyer questions like brand versus competitor, and creating a brand entity page with specific trust signals such as reviews, certifications, and awards. These changes make content directly extractable by AI engines and typically show improvement in 4 to 8 weeks.
