Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my brand recommended in ChatGPT?

ChatGPT tends to recommend brands it can read about consistently across trusted sources: reviews, comparison pages, community discussions and your own clear, well-structured pages. Start by checking what ChatGPT says about you today, find where competitors are cited instead, and build presence in those sources.

Does claiming my business in ChatGPT Business Hub improve recommendations?

Not directly. OpenAI says claiming lets you manage the information you provide but does not guarantee more visibility or control ChatGPT's independent recommendations. It is still worth doing for accurate facts and first-party insights.

Do ChatGPT Ads affect organic answers?

OpenAI states that advertising does not influence ChatGPT's independent answers and that sponsored placements are labeled and kept separate.

How do I measure my visibility in ChatGPT?

Combine Business Hub Insights (website references and clicks in ChatGPT) with a fixed panel of buyer questions run regularly across ChatGPT and other assistants, and with AI referral traffic in your analytics.
