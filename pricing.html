Pricing FAQ

How much does Jeevan AI cost?

Jeevan AI has a free forever plan at no cost. Paid plans are coming soon: Hobby at ₹2,500/month (incl. GST), Starter at ₹5,000/month, Growth at ₹15,000/month, and Agency at ₹40,000/month. For customers outside India: $29, $49, $149, and $399 per month plus applicable tax.

Is there a free version of Jeevan AI?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever and requires no credit card. It tracks 8 questions across ChatGPT and Gemini, runs 1 scan per month, gives you 1 article generation per month, 5 priority pages, 25 chat insight queries, and covers 1 brand.

What is the difference between the plans?

Free: 8 questions, ChatGPT + Gemini, 1 scan/month, 1 brand. Hobby: 25 questions, 2 scans. Starter: 50 questions, adds Perplexity, 4 articles/month. Growth: 75 questions/brand, adds Claude (all 4 platforms), 3 scans, 2 brands. Agency: 100 questions/brand, 4 brands.

Are the INR prices inclusive of GST?

Yes. All INR prices shown are inclusive of GST. USD prices are for customers outside India and are subject to applicable local tax.
