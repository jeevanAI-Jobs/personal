Questions about tracking ChatGPT and Perplexity referral traffic in GA4

Why AI traffic shows as direct, how to set up channel grouping, and how to measure true AI-driven demand.

How do I track ChatGPT referral traffic in GA4?

ChatGPT traffic appears as referral sessions from chat.openai.com in GA4. Find it under Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic Acquisition, filtered by session source. Create a custom channel grouping that consolidates all major AI platforms into one channel:

  1. Go to GA4 Admin → Data Display → Channel Groups
  2. Create a new channel group called "AI Referral"
  3. Add session source conditions for: chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, claude.ai, bing.com/chat
  4. Save. The grouping applies to new sessions immediately, not historical data.

Why does Perplexity and ChatGPT traffic show up as direct in GA4?

Some AI interfaces strip the referrer header before the click. When the referrer is missing, GA4 classifies the session as direct. This is especially common with Perplexity on mobile, ChatGPT's browsing mode, and some in-app AI surfaces. The real volume of AI-driven traffic is therefore higher than your referral report shows. The gap between AI referral sessions and actual AI-driven behavior is called AI dark traffic.

The best way to estimate the true total is to track branded search volume alongside AI referral sessions. If branded searches rise in proportion to when AI referral traffic rises, the difference is likely the unattributed direct portion from AI sources.

What is AI dark traffic and how large is it?

AI dark traffic is AI-driven visits that arrive at your site but are not attributed to an AI source in GA4. Instead they appear as direct sessions. There is no industry-standard measurement for the dark traffic percentage, but in B2B SaaS categories the unattributed share can be 30 to 60 percent of total AI-driven visits depending on the AI platform and device mix. Perplexity mobile and some ChatGPT surfaces are the largest contributors to dark traffic.

Does AI referral traffic convert better than organic search traffic?

In most B2B categories, AI referral traffic converts at significantly higher rates than organic search traffic. Users arriving from ChatGPT or Perplexity have typically already had a recommendation conversation with the AI and arrive at your site with a formed intent. They are further along in the consideration stage than a user who clicked a blue link. Session duration, pages per session, and lead form completion rates are typically higher for AI referral traffic. This makes it one of the highest-quality acquisition channels even at relatively low volume.

What is the difference between AI referral traffic and AI-influenced traffic?

AI referral traffic is sessions where GA4 can trace the source to a specific AI platform like chat.openai.com. AI-influenced traffic is broader: it includes users who saw your brand cited in an AI answer but arrived later via a direct visit, a branded Google search, or a social channel, after the attribution chain was broken by time or channel switching. AI-influenced traffic is structurally invisible in GA4. Branded search volume trend is the best available proxy for total AI-influenced demand.

How do I measure AI Share of Voice if AI traffic does not show in GA4?

AI Share of Voice is measured on the output side, not the traffic side. Instead of counting inbound sessions, you measure how often your brand appears in AI-generated responses for a defined set of queries in your category, compared to competitors. This requires querying AI platforms directly with your target queries and tracking citation rates over time.

Should AI referral traffic be my primary KPI for AI search performance?

No. AI referral sessions in GA4 measure only the attributed, click-through portion of AI-driven behavior. Because of dark traffic, a significant portion of AI-driven visits is invisible in GA4. The better primary KPI is AI Share of Voice: how often your brand appears in AI responses for your category's key queries. AI referral traffic is a useful secondary signal that confirms your AI visibility is generating real downstream demand, but it will always undercount total AI impact.
