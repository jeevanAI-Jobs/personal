AI Search Market Share 2026: Where B2B Buyers Are Actually Researching

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews are all growing simultaneously. Here is the data on where B2B buyers spend their research time and what it means for your visibility strategy.

This report covers AI search platform usage data for 2026, with a focus on B2B buyer behavior. It includes platform-by-platform breakdowns, buyer persona segmentation, and a priority framework for B2B brands deciding where to invest visibility efforts.

The question "where should we optimize for AI search?" has a different answer depending on who your buyer is. A developer-led product and an HR software platform have different buyer personas who use different AI tools in fundamentally different ways. The mistake most brands make is treating AI search as a single channel.

In 2026, AI search is a fragmented landscape. Google AI Overviews reach the largest audience. ChatGPT handles the most complex research conversations. Perplexity attracts the highest-intent researchers. Gemini is growing inside Google Workspace environments. Each platform has different citation mechanics, different buyer demographics, and different implications for visibility strategy.

This report assembles available data from SparkToro, SimilarWeb, and primary research to give B2B marketing teams a working picture of the AI search landscape in 2026.

AI Search in 2026: The Overall Picture

Traditional Google search still handles the vast majority of search volume globally. Google processes an estimated 8.5 billion searches per day. AI-native search tools are growing rapidly but from a smaller base.

The more relevant question for B2B brands is not total search volume but research-intent query volume. When buyers specifically want to evaluate a software category, compare vendors, or understand a technical concept, AI tools have captured a disproportionate share of those sessions compared to their overall search volume.

What This Means for Your Visibility Strategy

The fragmentation of AI search creates both a problem and an opportunity. The problem: you cannot rely on a single platform. The opportunity: the content and authority signals that help on one platform help on all of them.

The unified principle: Brands that are well-cited in AI search have three things in common regardless of which platform you check: they are clearly categorized in third-party sources (G2, Capterra, industry media), they have structured content that directly answers buyer questions, and they have consistent brand description language across all surfaces. These are not platform-specific tactics. They are foundational brand hygiene that compounds across every AI channel simultaneously.

To measure your current position across platforms, start with a manual AI visibility audit. Query your top 10 category keywords on each platform and document where your brand appears and where competitors appear instead. This baseline tells you where the gaps are before you invest in fixing them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AI search market share in 2026?

Google maintains over 90% of traditional search volume globally, but AI-native search tools have a disproportionate share of research-intent queries. ChatGPT handles 100M+ daily active users for search-like queries, Perplexity processes 100M+ queries per month, and Google AI Overviews appear on 15-25% of all Google searches. For B2B vendor research specifically, AI-native tools have captured a significant and growing portion of research sessions.

Do B2B buyers use ChatGPT for vendor research?

Yes. Multiple surveys from 2025 to 2026 show that 40 to 60 percent of B2B software buyers now use ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini at some point in their vendor evaluation process. The most common use cases are generating comparison lists, understanding a category, and asking follow-up questions that would otherwise require multiple Google searches.

Which AI search engine should B2B brands prioritize?

For most B2B SaaS brands in 2026, the priority order is: Google AI Overviews first (largest reach), ChatGPT second (most used for complex research), Perplexity third (fastest growing, high-intent research), and Gemini fourth (strong in Google Workspace environments). The exact order depends on your category and buyer persona.

Is Perplexity AI growing faster than ChatGPT?

Perplexity is growing faster from a smaller base, at roughly 300 percent year over year. ChatGPT is larger in absolute terms but growing more slowly on a percentage basis. For brand visibility, Perplexity is notable because it cites sources inline with visible links, making it a particularly high-value citation target per impression.
