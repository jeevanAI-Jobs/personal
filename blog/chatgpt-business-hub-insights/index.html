Business Hub Insights Explained: What Mentions, Clicks and Discovery Topics Really Tell You

ChatGPT now gives brands first-party numbers. They are valuable and narrow at the same time. Here is exactly what each one counts, what it misses, and a system to turn them into decisions.

Part of the ChatGPT Visibility Hub. New to Business Hub? Start with the complete setup guide linked below.

Short version. Business Hub Insights shows three aggregated numbers for the past 28 days: mentions, website clicks and discovery topics. For online businesses, a "mention" only counts when ChatGPT references your website, not when it names your brand. You never see prompts, conversations or competitors, and blanks are not zeros. Used with your own weekly log, analytics and a buyer-question panel, the numbers can show which topics already earn citations and where to build next. Used alone, they may badly understate how ChatGPT talks about you.

For most of the history of AI search, every visibility number a brand had was an estimate from outside: tools asking ChatGPT sample questions and counting answers. With Businesses in ChatGPT, OpenAI now reports activity from its own systems to verified businesses. That is a genuine shift, and the first instinct of many teams will be to put "ChatGPT mentions" straight into a monthly report.

Before you do, read the definitions. OpenAI has been careful about what these numbers mean, and the gaps between what they count and what marketers assume they count are large enough to change decisions.

What Insights Shows, in OpenAI's Own Definitions

Insights becomes available after your claim and Persona identity verification are both approved. You open your business in Business Hub and select Insights. OpenAI's help center describes it as aggregate activity over the past 28 days:

MetricWhat OpenAI countsWhat that implies
Mentions (online business)ChatGPT references the business's website. The brand name alone does not count.A measure of citation of your site, not of brand presence in answers
Mentions (local business)ChatGPT shows a result for that location, even if nobody opens it.Closer to an impression count
Website clicksHow often people click through to your website.Recorded in ChatGPT; not the same as a visit in your analytics
Discovery topicsCommon themes in ChatGPT answers that mentioned or cited your business.Themes of answers, not prompts, and not a direct measure of intent

OpenAI adds three conditions that matter for interpretation: insights are aggregated, they only appear when there are enough conversations to meet privacy thresholds, and counts "may be incomplete."

Key definition: In Business Hub, a mention for an online business means ChatGPT referenced your website. If ChatGPT recommends your brand by name but cites a review site, a marketplace or a news article instead, that recommendation does not appear in your mention count.

What Insights Leaves Out

  • Prompts and conversations. Businesses never receive individual prompts or consumer conversations, including chats with their own Business Agent.
  • Brand-name-only appearances. For online businesses, naming without a website reference records nothing.
  • Competitors. No share of voice, no list of who else appeared in the same answers.
  • How you were described. No sentiment, position in the answer, or accuracy check.
  • Which other sources were cited alongside or instead of your site.
  • Other AI engines. Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot and Google's AI surfaces are outside its scope.
  • Long history. The window is the past 28 days. Anything older is gone unless you record it.
  • Small numbers. Activity below the reporting threshold shows as blank.

None of this is a criticism. OpenAI designed Insights to protect user privacy and says so plainly. It simply means Insights is one instrument, not the dashboard of record for AI visibility.

The Citation Gap: The Most Useful Number Is One You Calculate

Because mentions only count website references, the most informative signal comes from comparing Insights with how often ChatGPT names you at all. Run a fixed panel of real buyer questions in ChatGPT (manually or with a tracker) and record two things per answer: was the brand named, and was your website cited.

Pattern you seeWhat it may meanWhere to act
Often named, rarely citedChatGPT knows you from third-party sources, but your own pages are not the reference it usesBuild pages that answer the exact questions buyers ask; improve structure and schema
Rarely named, sometimes citedYour site is used as a source for facts, but you are not on shortlistsEarn presence in reviews, comparisons and community discussion
Named and citedStrong position for those topicsProtect it: keep facts current, extend to adjacent topics
NeitherNot yet part of ChatGPT's picture of the categoryStart with entity basics and third-party coverage

Two simple ratios help track this over time:

  • Click rate = website clicks / mentions. A falling rate with steady mentions may point to weak landing pages for the topics you are cited on, or answers that satisfy users without a click.
  • Citation share of your panel = panel answers citing your site / panel answers naming you. Rising share suggests your own content is becoming the reference.

Blank Metrics: A Decision Tree

OpenAI states that a blank can mean the reporting threshold was not met, data is still being collected, or reporting is unavailable, and that it does not necessarily mean zero. Work through it in order:

  1. Is Persona approved? Insights stay locked until it is. Check the profile overview for "Complete verification with Persona".
  2. Was the profile verified recently? Data may still be collecting. Wait at least one full 28-day window before drawing conclusions.
  3. Is your volume likely small? If your panel shows ChatGPT rarely cites your site, you are probably below the privacy threshold. Record "below threshold", not zero.
  4. Is one metric blank and another not? Clicks can fall below threshold while mentions do not. Treat each separately.
  5. Still blank after weeks of real traffic in analytics? Contact OpenAI Support with the step, any error and a screenshot with sensitive information removed.

Reporting rule: never write a blank into a report as 0. Write "not reported (below threshold or pending)". A zero that later becomes a number looks like growth that did not happen.

Build a Weekly Log Before Anything Else

Because Insights is a rolling 28-day view, the only way to see a trend is to record it yourself. Take a reading on the same weekday each week:

The last column is the one teams skip and later regret. Without it, you cannot connect a change in the numbers to anything you did.

Reconciling Business Hub Clicks With GA4

Business Hub records clicks inside ChatGPT. GA4 records sessions that load your tag and are attributed to a source. The two will rarely match, and that is expected. Common reasons a recorded click does not show up as a ChatGPT session in analytics:

  • The visitor declined analytics cookies, or a blocker stopped the tag.
  • A redirect (for example http to https, or a country redirect) dropped the referrer.
  • The click opened in an app's in-app browser that passed no referrer.
  • The landing page loaded slowly and the visitor left before the tag fired.
  • Your channel grouping does not recognise ChatGPT as a source, so sessions sit under Referral or Direct.

Setup that makes the comparison meaningful

  1. Create a custom channel group in GA4 with an "AI assistants" channel matching sources such as chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com and other assistants you care about. Place it above Referral so it wins.
  2. Build a landing page report filtered to that channel. Pages that receive ChatGPT traffic are usually the pages ChatGPT cites.
  3. Compare the same 28-day window as Business Hub, and track the ratio over time rather than the gap on any single day.
  4. Treat Business Hub clicks as the closer-to-source number and GA4 as the number connected to conversions. You need both.

Turning Discovery Topics Into a Content Plan

Discovery topics are the most strategic part of Insights, and the easiest to misuse. OpenAI describes them as common themes in ChatGPT answers that mentioned or cited your business. They are not the questions people typed, and they do not directly measure what customers wanted. They tell you which subjects ChatGPT already associates with your site.

A practical method:

  1. List each topic with its share of mentions from your weekly log.
  2. Map it to the page most likely being cited. Use your GA4 landing page report for the AI channel to confirm.
  3. Classify it: strong (a dedicated, detailed page exists), thin (only a passing mention), or missing (no page).
  4. Act by class: refresh strong pages with current facts; expand thin pages into full answers; create pages for adjacent topics buyers ask about.
  5. Compare with your target topics. The topics you want to be known for but do not see are the real gap list.

Using Insights to Fix Your Profile and Feed

  • Clicks fall while mentions hold: check that your profile's website field and the pages being cited still load fast and answer the topic directly.
  • Mentions concentrate in one topic: your description may be too narrow. Make sure the profile description covers every core line you sell, in plain category terms.
  • A topic you no longer offer keeps appearing: update the cited page and your profile, then fix third-party pages still describing the old offer.
  • Product topics appear but clicks are low: confirm feed prices and availability match your site. OpenAI notes changes can take time to show.

Agent Insights

If you run a Business Agent, the same privacy rule applies: no individual chats or transcripts. For Sponsored Business Agents in Ads Manager, OpenAI says the Insights tab can show patterns across conversations, such as the most common questions, where available, and that an insights agent can help explore them once an agent passes 25 conversations. Treat recurring questions as a list of pages your website should answer more clearly.

Filling the Gaps Insights Cannot Cover

Insights answers "how often did ChatGPT reference our site, and did people click". The questions leadership usually asks are broader: are we on the shortlist, who is recommended instead, what do answers say about us, and is it the same in other assistants. Those need an outside view:

  • A fixed panel of buyer questions run regularly across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Mode (multi-LLM monitoring).
  • The sources each answer cites, so you know where to earn coverage (AI citations vs backlinks).
  • Competitor presence in the same answers (competitive AI brand audit).
  • Accuracy checks on how you are described (fixing wrong AI descriptions).

When the outside view and Insights disagree, the disagreement is itself a finding. Many named appearances but few recorded mentions suggests ChatGPT trusts other sources about you more than your own site.

How to Report It to Leadership

Use labels that match what the number counts. A monthly summary might read:

ChatGPT website references (Business Hub, last 28 days): 412, up from 350.
Clicks to our site from ChatGPT: 96 (click rate 23%). GA4 shows 71 AI-assistant sessions over the same window.
Top cited themes: sunscreen for oily skin (44%), returns and delivery (18%).
Buyer-question panel (40 questions, 5 assistants): named in 31%, our site cited in 12%.
Interpretation: ChatGPT appears to rely on third-party pages when it names us. Next month: two new guides for missing themes, and review outreach.
Figures are illustrative.

Avoid "AI visibility up 18%" when the number is website references in one assistant. Precise labels protect the credibility of the whole program.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a mention in Business Hub?

For an online business, a mention counts when ChatGPT references the business's website; the brand name alone does not count. For a local business, a mention counts when ChatGPT shows a result for that location, even if nobody opens it.

Why is my insight blank?

OpenAI says a blank can mean the reporting threshold was not met, data is still being collected, or reporting is unavailable. It does not necessarily mean zero.

Can I see the prompts people used?

No. Businesses do not receive prompts or conversations, including chats with their own agent. Discovery topics are aggregate themes of answers.

How far back does the data go?

The past 28 days. Keep a weekly log if you want history.

Why do Business Hub clicks not match GA4?

Business Hub records clicks in ChatGPT; GA4 records attributed sessions that load your tag. Consent, blockers, redirects, in-app browsers and channel grouping all create gaps. Compare trends, not single numbers.

Do Insights show competitors?

No. There is no share of voice or list of other businesses in the same answers. Use a buyer-question panel or an AI visibility tool for that.
