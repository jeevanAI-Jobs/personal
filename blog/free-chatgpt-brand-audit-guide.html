How to Audit Your Brand on ChatGPT for Free (Step-by-Step)

Why a Manual AI Audit Still Matters

Before investing in any AI visibility tool, a quick manual audit tells you whether you have a problem worth solving. Many teams skip this step and either over-invest in optimisation or miss obvious gaps that a 20-minute check would have caught.

A free manual audit covers three things: whether AI mentions your brand at all, what it says when it does, and which competitors it recommends instead. That is enough to know where to start.

The goal of this audit is not to get a score. It is to understand the narrative AI currently tells about your brand in your buyers' key purchase scenarios.

Step 1: Build Your Query Set

Do not search for your brand name. Search for what your buyers search for. Think in buyer intent, not brand terms.

For each of these four query types, write two to three versions using your product category:

Query TypeExample
Best-in-category"best AI visibility tools for B2B SaaS"
Problem-led"my brand does not show up in ChatGPT, how do I fix it"
Competitor comparison"alternatives to [competitor name]"
Use case specific"AI visibility tool for D2C brands in India"

Run each query across ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Gemini, and Perplexity. Use fresh incognito sessions to avoid personalisation bias.

Step 2: Record What You See

For each query, note four things in a simple spreadsheet:

  • Is your brand mentioned? Yes, No, or Only in passing
  • What position? First named, listed among many, or buried at the end
  • What is said about you? Copy the exact description AI gives of your brand
  • Who else is mentioned? Note competitor names and their descriptions
Pay close attention to how AI describes your brand versus how you describe yourself. Gaps here reveal how your published content is being interpreted by AI systems.

Step 3: Score Your Results

After running all queries, assess five dimensions that AI systems weigh when deciding which brand to recommend:

FactorWhat to Look ForRed Flag
Use Case CoverageDoes AI match you to the right problem?Mentioned for generic queries only
Positioning AccuracyDoes AI describe your product correctly?Wrong category, outdated feature set
Trust SignalsDoes AI cite reviews, case studies, or data points?Described vaguely with no evidence
Pricing ClarityDoes AI mention your pricing tier or range?Priced incorrectly or price not mentioned
Competitor GapAre competitors mentioned in queries where you are not?Competitor named; your brand absent

Step 4: Identify Your Three Biggest Gaps

From your audit, list the three queries where the gap between your brand and the top-mentioned competitor is largest. These are your first content priorities.

For each gap, ask: what content does our competitor have published that we do not? The answer is almost always a case study, a use-case landing page, or a detailed comparison post.

Step 5: Decide Whether to Automate

A manual audit takes around 30 to 45 minutes and gives you a snapshot. The limitation is that it captures one moment in time and covers only the queries you think to test.

Automated tracking runs the same query set weekly across all major AI platforms, tracks position changes over time, and alerts you when a competitor gains or loses mentions. For teams running more than one brand or more than 20 tracked queries, the manual approach becomes impractical within a few months.

What to Do With Your Audit Results

The most common finding from a first audit is that AI describes your brand accurately for broad category queries but misses your specific use cases. The fix is publishing detailed use-case content, not homepage rewrites.

If AI mentions competitors in queries where you do not appear at all, you have a coverage gap. The fastest fix is a comparison post that directly addresses the query, names the competing options, and clearly explains the scenario where your product is the right choice.

If AI describes your brand incorrectly, the fix is publishing clear, structured, authoritative content that corrects the record. FAQ schema, structured product descriptions, and third-party coverage all help AI systems update their understanding of your brand over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Open ChatGPT in an incognito window and run queries that match your buyers' purchase decisions, not your brand name. Test best-in-category queries, problem-led queries, and competitor comparison queries. Note whether your brand is mentioned, what position it appears in, and what AI says about it. Repeat the same queries on Gemini and Perplexity for a complete picture.
