How to Set Up a ChatGPT Ad Campaign Step by Step (2026)

Sep 20, 2026 · 12 min read

OpenAI's self-serve Ads Manager is live. Getting a campaign running takes about 30 minutes — but there are five decisions along the way that determine whether your ChatGPT ads generate any results or just burn the $25 daily minimum. This is the complete setup walkthrough, with the decisions explained.

Before You Start — What You Need

Before creating an Ads Manager account, have these ready:

  • A business email address (personal Gmail accounts may be flagged for verification)
  • Your brand's domain — you will need to verify ownership
  • A payment method — credit card or approved business payment method
  • At least one landing page URL that is live and functional
  • Your three to five Context Hints drafted (more on this below — this is the most important pre-work)
  • Your ad creative written — headline (16 chars max), description (32 chars max)

The creative constraint is real: 16 characters for a headline is approximately two short words or one longer brand name. Write your creative before you open the Ads Manager — most teams underestimate how hard the character limits are until they are sitting in the setup flow with no working copy.

Notice there is no room for a value proposition, a feature list, or any nuance. Every character is load-bearing. Test at least 2 headline and description variants to find what gets the click.

Writing Context Hints — The Most Important Step

Context Hints are the targeting mechanism for ChatGPT ads. They are semantic descriptions of the conversation contexts you want to appear in — not keywords. This is the decision that has the highest impact on whether your budget reaches the right users.

How to write Context Hints that work:

  • Describe the buyer's problem, not your product. "Someone asking how to track which AI systems are recommending their competitors" — not "AI visibility platform."
  • Be specific about the situation. "A conversation where someone is researching why their brand is not appearing in ChatGPT responses" outperforms "AI marketing."
  • Include the buyer's role or context if relevant. "A marketing manager at a B2B SaaS company asking about brand visibility in AI tools."
  • Write 3 to 5 hints, not 1 or 20. Too few and the system has limited eligible contexts. Too many and you dilute your signal.

Context Hints vs keywords: if you think of Context Hints as keywords, you will write the wrong things. "CRM software" is a keyword. "Someone asking for help choosing CRM software for a 20-person sales team" is a Context Hint. The second one describes a conversation — which is what ChatGPT's targeting system matches against.

The Setup Steps — In Order

1
Create your Ads Manager account

Go to ads.openai.com and sign up with your business email. You will go through identity verification and may need to accept advertiser policies. Domain verification is required — connect your brand domain to confirm you are advertising for a legitimate business.

2
Set up payment

Add a credit card or approved payment method. The system will confirm the $25 daily minimum at this point. Set your billing threshold — the amount at which the system charges your card mid-cycle — to avoid surprise charges.

3
Create your first campaign and ad set

Campaign structure mirrors other ad platforms: Campaign (objective) > Ad Set (targeting and budget) > Ad (creative). Choose your campaign objective. For most brand awareness purposes, "traffic" or "awareness" objectives are appropriate. Pipeline-focused campaigns may use "conversion" objectives if you have pixel tracking in place.

4
Enter your Context Hints

In the ad set targeting section, enter your 3 to 5 Context Hints. Write each one as a complete sentence describing a conversation context. The system shows a preview of how many conversations your hints are estimated to match — use this as a signal for whether you are too broad or too specific.

5
Set budget and oCPC target

Set your daily budget (minimum $25). Set your target CPC — $3 to $5 is the current market range for most categories. Start conservative: $3 to $4 target CPC for the first 2 weeks while the system calibrates, then adjust based on click volume.

6
Create your ad creative

Enter your headline (max 16 characters), description (max 32 characters), and destination URL. Create 2 to 3 variants — different headlines on the same destination URL is a valid starting point. The system will rotate variants and weight toward the better-performing one after sufficient click data.

7
Set up conversion tracking

Add UTM parameters to your destination URL before launch — at minimum, utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=[your campaign name]. If you have access to the OpenAI pixel, install it for more granular conversion tracking. Without tracking, you will know how many clicks you got but not how many converted — which makes it impossible to evaluate actual ROI.

8
Submit for review and launch

OpenAI reviews ads before they go live — typically 24 to 48 hours. Once approved, your ads enter delivery. Do not make changes to Context Hints or creative in the first 14 days — let the oCPC system calibrate without interruption. Review performance at the 14-day and 30-day marks.

After Launch — What to Watch and When to Optimise

The most common mistake new ChatGPT advertisers make is optimising too early. The oCPC system needs 2 to 3 weeks of data to calibrate which conversation contexts produce clicks that match your target CPC. Changing Context Hints or bids in week one resets the calibration and extends the learning period.

After 14 to 21 days, review these metrics:

  • Impression share: if low, your Context Hints may be too specific — widen them
  • Click-through rate: below 0.5% suggests creative is not resonating in context — test different headlines
  • Actual CPC vs target: consistently above target suggests high competition for your contexts — lower the target or broaden hints
  • Landing page conversion rate (via UTM/pixel): below 1% suggests audience-landing page mismatch — the context is attracting users whose intent does not match your landing page offer

The organic GEO benchmark: before making significant ChatGPT Ads budget increases, benchmark your organic AI presence. If ChatGPT and Gemini are already recommending your brand organically for your target queries, paid ads in those same contexts may compete with your own organic citations rather than supplementing them. In my experience with AI visibility audits, brands with strong organic AI presence often achieve better engagement metrics from organic citations than from paid placements on the same platform — which changes the ROI case for paid spend significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you access OpenAI's Ads Manager?

OpenAI's Ads Manager is at ads.openai.com. You create a separate account from your ChatGPT user account using a business email. After account creation, you need to verify your brand domain, connect a payment method, and accept advertiser policies before launching a campaign.

What are Context Hints and how do you write them?

Context Hints are semantic descriptions of the conversation contexts you want your ad to appear in. They describe buyer problems and research situations, not product features. "Someone asking how to manage a growing sales team" is a Context Hint. "CRM software" is a keyword — not a Context Hint. Write 3 to 5 complete sentences describing the conversations where your ideal buyer is most likely to encounter your ad.

What are the creative character limits for ChatGPT ads?

Headline maximum 16 characters, description maximum 32 characters, plus a destination URL. These are extremely constraining compared to Google Ads or social media formats — approximately two short words for a headline and five to seven words for a description. Write your creative in advance before entering the Ads Manager setup flow.

How long does it take for ChatGPT ads to be approved?

OpenAI typically reviews and approves new ads within 24 to 48 hours. Changes to existing campaigns — new creative, modified Context Hints — also go through review. Factor this into your launch timeline if you have a specific date to hit.

When should I change my ChatGPT ad after launching?

Wait at least 14 to 21 days before making targeting or bid changes. The oCPC system needs this calibration period to optimise effectively. Making changes in week one resets the learning period. After 14 to 21 days, review impression share, CTR, actual CPC versus target, and landing page conversion rate — then make one change at a time to isolate what is working.
