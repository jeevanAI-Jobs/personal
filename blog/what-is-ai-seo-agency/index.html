What Is an AI SEO Agency and Do You Actually Need One?

What AI SEO agencies do, how they price, and an honest decision framework for whether your stage and budget justify the spend.

Short answer: An AI SEO agency helps brands improve their citation rate in AI-generated responses. Most early-stage B2B SaaS brands do not need one — a self-serve AI visibility platform and in-house execution covers 80% of the work at a fraction of the cost. Agencies are justified at Series B and beyond, or when you are in a highly competitive category and need to close a large citation gap fast.

A new agency category has emerged around the term "AI SEO." Pricing ranges from $2,500 to $20,000 per month. The services on offer vary significantly. Some agencies are former traditional SEO shops that have rebranded. Others are genuinely built around generative engine optimization — the practice of improving your brand's citation rate in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

Before spending on an agency, you need to understand exactly what the work involves and whether you can do it yourself. In most cases, you can — at least at the beginning.

What an AI SEO agency actually does

A legitimate AI SEO agency — one focused on generative engine optimization, not just AI-assisted content writing — typically delivers six categories of work:

  • ⚖
    AI visibility baseline audit
    Measuring your current citation rate across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for your category queries. Setting up your AI share of voice baseline versus named competitors. This is the starting point for everything else.
  • ◆
    Entity authority buildout
    Completing and standardizing your G2, Capterra, Crunchbase, and LinkedIn profiles with consistent category language. Adding Organization and SoftwareApplication schema to your website. This is the highest-impact work and takes 2 to 4 weeks to execute correctly.
  • ✍
    GEO content strategy and production
    Writing pages that answer the specific query types B2B buyers ask AI before shortlisting vendors. This includes FAQPage schema, structured definitions, comparison content, and validation content. The brief differs from a standard SEO brief.
  • 📰
    Press release distribution
    Drafting and distributing wire press releases (PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire) that create dozens of indexed mentions of your brand with consistent category language. Each release adds to the web's understanding of what your brand does.
  • 📊
    Ongoing citation monitoring
    Weekly or monthly tracking of your brand's AI citation rate across platforms, competitor share of voice, sentiment, and GEO audit scores. Reporting on what changed and why.
  • 👑
    Review platform campaigns
    Structured campaigns to increase G2 and Capterra review volume. Review count and recency are among the highest-signal inputs for AI category recommendations. This is systematic outreach to customers, not passive accumulation.

Do you need an agency or a self-serve tool?

The honest answer depends on three factors: your stage, your marketing team capacity, and how competitive your category is.

Self-serve tool + in-house

Right for most B2B SaaS brands

  • Pre-seed through Series A
  • Marketing team of 1 to 3 people
  • Category not yet highly competitive in AI
  • Willing to learn and execute GEO in-house
  • Budget under $2,000/month for AI visibility
AI SEO agency

Justified at later stages

  • Series B and beyond
  • Competitor is heavily cited and gap is large
  • Marketing team has no GEO expertise
  • Need results faster than in-house execution allows
  • Brand reputation is at stake in AI responses

The most efficient path for most teams: start with an AI visibility platform to get your baseline measurement, run the entity authority fixes in-house (they are not technically complex), and only bring in an agency if you hit a ceiling you cannot clear internally.

If your competitor is consistently recommended and you are not, and you have tried the in-house fixes without results after 3 months, that is the signal to bring in external help.

Red flags when evaluating AI SEO agencies

  • They promise specific citation placements or guarantee ChatGPT recommendations — no one can guarantee this
  • Their deliverables are AI-written blog posts rather than entity buildout and citation strategy
  • They cannot explain what AI share of voice is or how they measure it
  • They use traditional SEO metrics (rankings, DA, traffic) as their primary KPIs
  • They have no case studies showing before/after citation rate improvement
  • Their "AI SEO" offering is primarily about Google AI Overviews, not ChatGPT and Perplexity

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an AI SEO agency do?

An AI SEO agency improves your brand's citation rate in AI-generated responses. Core services include AI visibility audits, entity authority buildouts, GEO content production, press release distribution, G2 review campaigns, and ongoing monitoring of your AI share of voice versus competitors.

How much does an AI SEO agency cost?

Monthly retainers run $2,500 to $20,000 depending on scope. One-time project engagements run $1,500 to $25,000. For most early-stage B2B SaaS brands, a self-serve AI visibility platform at $200 to $800 per month combined with in-house execution delivers better ROI than an agency retainer.

Can an AI SEO agency guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my brand?

No. AI systems do not accept paid placements or direct submissions. A good agency improves the signals that increase citation probability — entity authority, third-party coverage, content structure — but cannot guarantee specific citations.

What is the difference between an AI SEO agency and an AI SEO tool?

An AI SEO tool measures your citation rate and share of voice. An agency executes the work to improve it. Most teams need both: the tool for measurement and the agency or in-house team for execution.
