What You Actually Need at This Budget

At the under-Rs-5,000 bracket, the realistic baseline is: tracked queries across two to three AI platforms, a regular scan schedule, and some form of competitor comparison. You are unlikely to get white-label reporting, API access, or multi-brand dashboards at this price point, and that is usually fine for a growth-stage team testing the value of AI visibility work.

The most important things to verify before choosing any tool at this price: how many queries are actually tracked per scan, which AI platforms are covered, and whether the data is fresh or cached from days ago.

A tool that tracks 8 queries on two platforms gives you directional data. A tool that tracks 50 queries across three platforms gives you actionable data. The difference in cost is often less than Rs 2,500 per month.

What the Market Looks Like

Most global AI visibility platforms are priced in USD and optimised for US and European enterprise buyers. At typical INR-USD exchange rates, entry-level plans from global tools often land in the Rs 4,000 to Rs 8,000 range for very limited query sets.

India-first tools are priced differently because the cost structure is different and the buyer profile is different. A platform built for Indian growth-stage teams can offer more queries and more frequent scans at a lower price than a US-origin tool can without losing margin.

When Free Is Enough

If you are at the very beginning of AI visibility work and want to understand the problem before committing budget, a free plan gives you enough data to validate whether a gap exists. Eight tracked queries across two platforms will tell you if your brand appears at all for its primary buying queries and whether a named competitor is consistently outranking you.

The limitation of free plans is frequency. A single monthly scan is a snapshot, not a trend. If you are actively publishing content and want to track whether it is improving your AI visibility, you need at minimum a bi-monthly scan cycle.

The Rs 2,500 Sweet Spot

For most Indian growth-stage teams, the Rs 2,500 per month tier is the practical entry point for real AI visibility work. At this price, you get enough tracked queries to cover your primary buying scenarios, bi-monthly scans to track progress, and historical data to show your team that the work is moving the needle.

This is also the point where competitor tracking becomes meaningful. Seeing which competitor is named in queries where you are absent, and watching that change over time as you publish more targeted content, is the core feedback loop that makes AI visibility work sustainable.

What Rs 5,000 Adds

The Rs 5,000 tier adds Perplexity as a third platform, increases the tracked query count to 50, and typically includes content brief generation. For teams actively publishing content based on AI visibility gaps, the content brief feature alone can justify the price difference over the Rs 2,500 tier.

Perplexity matters more than its market share might suggest. Perplexity is disproportionately used by B2B researchers and technically sophisticated buyers, the segment most likely to be making a high-value purchase decision. If your product is above Rs 1 lakh per year, Perplexity visibility is worth tracking.

What to Avoid at This Budget

Be cautious of tools that advertise large query counts but run those queries infrequently, cache results for extended periods, or aggregate responses in ways that reduce accuracy. A tool tracking 200 queries monthly from data that is four weeks old is less useful than a tool tracking 25 queries weekly from live AI responses.

Also check whether the tool supports INR billing with GST invoices. For Indian companies, tools that only bill in USD create accounting friction and sometimes a meaningful cost premium at current exchange rates.

Making the Decision

Start with the free plan if you have never run an AI visibility audit. The data from a single free scan will either confirm that your brand has a gap worth addressing or tell you that your current position is already reasonable and the priority should be elsewhere.

Move to a paid plan when: you have confirmed a gap exists, you are actively publishing content to fix it, and you need scan frequency high enough to measure whether the content is working. At that point, the Rs 2,500 tier is typically sufficient until you have more than two brands or need more than 25 tracked queries to cover your full buying scenario set.
