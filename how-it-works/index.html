From invisible to recommended, in three steps

Most AI visibility tools give you a gap report and a content brief. Jeevan AI gives you the full content draft, ready to publish. Here is exactly how it works.

The process

Three steps, one outcome: AI engines recommend you

Every Jeevan AI scan is built around a single goal: ending with a publishable action, not a dashboard to stare at.

1

Run a scan

Enter your brand URL. Jeevan AI discovers your brand details, auto-detects competitors, and runs ~25 buyer queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Mode. You get a separate score for each platform so you know where you appear and where you are being skipped.

2

Get full content drafts

Every gap is diagnosed by the specific buying factor causing it. Then Jeevan AI generates the full content draft for the page that would close it, grounded in your Knowledge Base and social signal data. Not a brief. Not a checklist. A complete, publishable page.

3

Publish and track

Publish the draft on your site. Re-Scan after a few weeks to measure the change in your citation score per platform and per buying factor. The loop continues until you appear where you should.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Jeevan AI give me a full content draft or just a brief?
Jeevan AI gives you a full content draft. A brief tells you what to write. A draft is already written. You can take the draft, review it, and publish it on your site without needing a separate writer. SEO meta, schema markup, and JSON-LD are included in every exported draft.
How does Jeevan AI know what to put in the draft?
The draft is generated from your scan results (which buying factor is causing the gap), your Knowledge Base (the specific facts and claims about your brand that you upload), and your social signal data (what buyers are saying about your category on Reddit and in Google signals). The output is specific to your brand, not a generic template.
How long does a scan take?
A full scan across all five AI platforms typically takes 3 to 8 minutes. The scan runs ~25 buyer queries per platform, scores each buying factor, and returns a gap analysis with content drafts. You do not need to stay on the page while it runs.
Do I need to publish through Jeevan AI?
No. You can export the draft and publish it anywhere: your own CMS, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, or any other platform. Direct publish integration is coming soon and will allow you to publish the fix directly from Jeevan AI without switching tools.
What is a buying factor and why does it matter?
A buying factor is one of the five criteria AI engines use to decide which brand to recommend: Trust, Use Case Fit, Pricing, Ease of Use, and Quality. Each gap in your scan is attributed to the specific buying factor causing it, so you know whether you need to add proof of quality, clarify your pricing, or address a specific use case. Without this diagnosis, all you know is that you did not appear. With it, you know exactly what to publish to change that.
