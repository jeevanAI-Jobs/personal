Skincare

How a mass-market skincare brand improved AI citation rates after fixing entity clarity

Tested on 4 AI platforms Indian D2C brand Brand anonymised
72
AI visibility score / 100

Situation

A widely distributed skincare brand available across major Indian e-commerce platforms was tested for AI visibility. The brand had strong retail penetration but its online entity information was inconsistent: ingredient lists, product descriptions and brand origin details differed across third-party retailer pages. In testing, this appeared to create ambiguity for AI platforms when assembling brand-level responses.

What Was Tested

The brand was tested across a set of queries covering skincare recommendations by concern (dry skin, oily skin, anti-ageing) and ingredient-based queries. Platform-level citation rates and the nature of mentions were recorded across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

All testing used Jeevan AI’s standard query set. Results reflect a specific point in time and may vary across sessions. This case study uses hedged language throughout to reflect the indirect nature of AI visibility measurement.

Platform Breakdown

ChatGPT cited

Cited consistently in recommendation queries. Entity clarity and Wikipedia presence appeared to be key factors.

Perplexity cited

Cited frequently. SEO rankings and structured content on retailer pages appeared to contribute.

Gemini cited

Cited in some queries. Google Business Profile completeness appeared correlated.

Google AI Overviews not cited

Not consistently triggered for this category. When triggered, the brand was not included in tested queries.

What the Testing Suggested

  • In testing, the brand was cited on 3 of 4 platforms, suggesting a reasonable baseline entity presence.
  • Tested queries on ChatGPT returned consistent citations, which may indicate that well-corroborated third-party sources were already present in its training data.
  • Perplexity citations appeared to track with the brand's retailer page rankings, suggesting that live web retrieval may amplify distribution-based visibility.
  • Gemini citations appeared less consistent, which in testing correlated with a partially-completed Google Business Profile.
  • Google AI Overviews did not trigger for most of the tested skincare queries, limiting the data available for that platform.

Signals That Appeared to Matter

  • Consistent entity information across retailer pages
  • Wikipedia or Wikidata entry with verified facts
  • Organisation schema on brand homepage
  • Ingredient and product data structured in a machine-readable format
  • Third-party editorial mentions beyond brand-owned content
  • Complete Google Business Profile

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI visibility for a skincare brand?
AI visibility refers to whether and how often a brand is cited or recommended when users ask skincare questions on AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini. It is distinct from SEO rankings and depends on how well AI models can identify and trust information about the brand.
Does retail distribution help AI visibility?
In testing, broader retail distribution may help indirectly by increasing the number of third-party pages that mention the brand. However, if those pages carry conflicting information, the net effect on AI visibility is unclear. Entity consistency appears to matter more than distribution alone.
How is AI visibility measured?
Jeevan AI measures AI visibility by running a standardised set of queries on each platform and recording whether the brand appears in responses, how it is described, and which factors appear correlated with citation. Scores are indicative and should be treated as directional rather than definitive.
