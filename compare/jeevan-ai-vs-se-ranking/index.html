Two Different Problems

This comparison is not about which tool is better. Jeevan AI and SE Ranking do not compete for the same job. SE Ranking is built to answer: "Where do I rank on Google for my target keywords?" Jeevan AI is built to answer: "Does ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini recommend my brand when a buyer asks a question in my category?"

Buyer research in B2B is splitting across two channels. Traditional search still drives significant traffic and evaluation. But a growing share of early-stage research — the queries buyers run before they even visit a vendor's website — is now happening in AI chat interfaces. A brand can rank on page one of Google and still be completely absent from AI-generated responses, and vice versa. Tracking only one channel gives an incomplete picture.

In our experience tracking B2B brand visibility across both channels, a brand's Google ranking for a keyword and its citation frequency for the equivalent AI query have almost no correlation. Brands at position 5 on Google can be the top AI-cited brand in the same category. Brands at position 1 can be absent from AI responses entirely. They are different signals driven by different content and authority factors.

SE Ranking's Agency Tools and What They Cover

SE Ranking has built a strong agency feature set including white-label reporting, a client portal, a lead generation widget, and multi-account dashboards. These tools are well regarded for agencies managing traditional SEO programs across multiple clients.

However, SE Ranking's agency tools are entirely oriented around traditional search engine data. An agency that wants to offer AI visibility tracking as part of its service offering will not find that capability in SE Ranking. As AI search becomes a recognized marketing channel, agencies are increasingly looking to add GEO monitoring alongside their existing SEO reporting. For that layer, a tool like Jeevan AI is additive rather than competitive with SE Ranking.

Summary

SE Ranking and Jeevan AI solve different problems. If you are only doing one thing, start with the channel where your buyers spend the most time researching. For most B2B brands in 2026, that answer is increasingly "both" — Google still drives significant research traffic, but the early-stage research that precedes a Google search is often now happening in ChatGPT or Perplexity.

If you already use SE Ranking for SEO and want to add AI visibility coverage, Jeevan AI sits alongside it without overlap. If you are evaluating your first marketing analytics tool, the right starting point depends on where your buyers research: traditional search, AI search, or both.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Jeevan AI and SE Ranking?
SE Ranking tracks website rankings in Google and other traditional search engines. Jeevan AI tracks how often your brand appears in AI-generated responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. They measure different channels: traditional search visibility versus AI search visibility. Most B2B teams need both as the two channels serve different stages of the buyer journey.
Does SE Ranking track AI brand citations?
No. SE Ranking does not track AI brand citations. It is built around traditional search engine ranking data. It does not monitor how often your brand appears in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini responses, does not measure AI Share of Voice, and does not provide GEO content recommendations. A dedicated GEO tool is required for AI citation tracking.
Can SE Ranking and Jeevan AI be used together?
Yes, and for most B2B marketing teams they should be used together. SE Ranking covers traditional search visibility — keyword rankings, technical SEO, backlinks. Jeevan AI covers AI search visibility — brand citations in AI responses, AI Share of Voice, and GEO content gaps. Together they give a complete picture of brand visibility across both channels.
Is SE Ranking good for agencies?
SE Ranking has a strong agency feature set: white-label reporting, client portals, multi-account dashboards, and lead generation widgets. It is well suited for agencies running traditional SEO programs. Agencies that want to add AI visibility tracking as a service offering will need a separate GEO monitoring tool, as SE Ranking does not cover AI citation tracking or GEO program management.
