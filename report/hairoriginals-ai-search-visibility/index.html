Why this score matters

The gap between HairOriginals' real-world brand strength and its AI visibility score is the clearest content investment signal in this audit.

Full breakdown
Entity Clarity58/100
HairOriginals has reasonable entity recognition as an Indian hair extension brand. AI engines associate it with scalp toppers, hair extensions, and human hair products. The entity signals for the Try At Home service and the 12-city experience center network are weaker: these distinguishing features are not currently structured in Organization schema or a dedicated About page that AI engines can extract and cite for location-specific or service-type queries.
Fix: Create an Organization schema entry naming HairOriginals, its service area (12 cities listed), and its core service differentiators: Try At Home consultation, 100% human hair, AI Try On. Add a dedicated Experience Centers page listing each city with address, contact number, and hours. This becomes the AI engine source for all location-specific queries: hair extensions store in Bengaluru, where to try hair patch in Delhi, hair extension salon near me Hyderabad.
Extractable Structure44/100
The HairOriginals website is structured primarily as a product catalogue and booking interface. Product category pages like Scalp Toppers, Volumizers, and Men's Patch have product listings but lack editorial sections with H2 headings explaining who each product type is for, how it is applied, and what the expected outcome is. AI engines extract primarily from structured text blocks, not from product grids.
Fix: Add a 200-word structured editorial section to each product category page. For Scalp Toppers: who they are designed for (women with diffuse thinning or alopecia areata), how they differ from full wigs, what the attachment method is, and what to expect in terms of appearance and maintenance. Use H2 subheadings. These blocks are what AI engines index when a buyer asks about a specific product type.
Schema Markup32/100
Schema markup implementation appears limited. Product schema may be present from the e-commerce platform but FAQPage schema, Organization schema with location data, LocalBusiness schema for experience centers, and HowTo schema for application guides are absent or inconsistently implemented. For a brand with 12 physical locations, LocalBusiness schema for each city would be a direct AI visibility lever.
Fix: Implement three schema types immediately. FAQPage on the homepage and each product category page. Organization schema with ServiceArea listing all 12 cities. LocalBusiness schema for each experience center location with address, phone, and opening hours. LocalBusiness schema is particularly valuable for location-specific queries from buyers in specific cities.
FAQ Coverage30/100
FAQ content is largely absent from the HairOriginals website. Buyer decision questions are unanswered in an extractable format: how to choose between a scalp topper and a wig, what the difference is between clip-in and tape-in extensions, whether the Try At Home service has a cost, how long hair extensions last with daily use, whether human hair extensions can be heat styled, and what the return policy is. In tested queries, these were answered by third-party beauty blogs and review sites rather than hairoriginals.com.
Fix: Publish a 30-question FAQ page covering five topics: Choosing the Right Product (topper vs wig vs extensions), The Try At Home Service (process, cost, cities available), Care and Maintenance, Quality and Hair Type, and Returns and Warranty. Add FAQPage schema markup. Each of these 30 questions should be the exact phrasing a buyer would put into an AI engine before deciding whether to book a trial.
Answer-Led Content42/100
A blog or educational content section with answer-led articles is limited. Buyers who use AI engines to research hair extensions before purchasing typically ask: what is a scalp topper and is it right for me, how long do human hair extensions last, what to expect when getting hair extensions in India, and can I swim with hair extensions. These questions are answered on beauty blogs and YouTube, not on hairoriginals.com.
Fix: Publish six answer-led articles targeting the highest-volume buyer queries: what is a scalp topper and who should use one in India, how to choose hair extensions for thin hair guide, clip-in vs tape-in vs permanent hair extensions for Indian hair, what to expect at a HairOriginals Try At Home appointment, how long do human hair extensions last with daily styling, and hair patch for men India complete buyer guide. Each must open with a direct answer to the question in the first sentence.
Specificity & Evidence76/100
HairOriginals has strong specificity signals: 12+ named experience center cities, specific product price points (Rs 2,699 to Rs 46,399), named celebrity endorsers, 100% human hair specification, and specific product dimensions like scalp topper sizes. These are the strongest specificity signals in this audit. The evidence layer is thin: no customer volume data, no satisfaction rate, and no before-after case studies with specific hair concern context.
Fix: Add a Results and Social Proof page: number of customers served, satisfaction rate from reviews, before-after cases organized by hair concern (diffuse thinning, alopecia areata, postpartum hair loss, men's crown thinning), and city-specific volume data if available. Link to verifiable review sources. This specificity evidence page would strengthen AI citation for outcome and trust queries.
