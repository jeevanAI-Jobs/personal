What AI engines see today

Traya is one of the more consistently surfaced hair care brands in our tested AI queries for hair loss treatment in India. Its personalized diagnostic model and the combination of Ayurvedic and dermatological approach generates distinctive content signals that AI engines recognize. The gap is in ingredient-level and comparison content: buyers who ask AI engines to compare Traya with Minoxidil, Arata, or Minimalist are not consistently routed to Traya's own pages.

Why this score matters
Traya has built an unusually strong AI visibility position for an Indian hair care brand by combining two things AI engines respond well to: a distinctive mechanism claim and a content library explaining that mechanism in depth. The personalized hair test model generates user-specific content pathways that create more indexed pages per buyer archetype than a standard product catalogue. In tested queries about hair loss treatment for Indian men and women, Traya surfaced more consistently than most D2C competitors. The gap shows up in two places: ingredient-level queries where buyers research what Traya products contain and why those ingredients work, and direct comparison queries where buyers want to know whether Traya or Minoxidil or a competitor is the better choice for their specific hair concern.

Content Gaps Costing AI Citations

3 gaps found
Why does ChatGPT recommend competitors instead of Traya for hair loss treatment India
In tested queries for hair loss treatment India and which brand is best for hair fall, Traya surfaced in some but not all query variations. Minoxidil-related queries routed to clinical sources. Ayurvedic hair loss queries sometimes routed to other brands. The comparison space is not owned by Traya's own content.
What to do: Publish a comprehensive hair loss treatment comparison: Traya versus Minoxidil, Traya versus Finasteride, Traya versus Ayurvedic-only approaches, and Traya versus supplement-only protocols. Frame each comparison around mechanism, evidence base, and buyer profile suitability. Add FAQPage schema. A brand that publishes the comparison content controls the comparison narrative in AI engine answers.
How can Traya measure which AI queries competitors are winning
This is the meta-level insight: Traya's marketing team cannot currently see in real time which ChatGPT and Perplexity queries are routing buyers to Minoxidil recommendations, dermatologist directories, or competitor brands instead of traya.in. Without this visibility, content investment is unfocused.
What to do: Jeevan AI's weekly tracking shows exactly which hair loss and hair care queries are being won by which brands and sources in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini each week. For Traya specifically, this would identify the comparison queries, the ingredient queries, and the outcome queries where content investment would most directly recover citation share.
Traya results how long does it take and success rate India
Results timeline and success rate are the highest-barrier pre-purchase questions for any subscription-model hair treatment. In tested queries, these questions were answered by review sites and user testimonials rather than Traya's own structured data pages.
What to do: Publish a results page: average time to first visible reduction in hair fall based on internal subscriber data, average time to new growth, success rate percentage with definition of success stated, and breakdown by hair loss type. State sample size and data collection methodology. This page would own the results query cluster for Traya and significantly reduce pre-purchase hesitation routed to competitor sites.

Full breakdown
Entity Clarity78/100
Traya has strong entity clarity as India's personalized hair loss treatment brand. AI engines consistently associate it with the combination hair care approach: Ayurvedic herbs plus dermatological actives plus nutrition. The entity is well established through media coverage and a distinct positioning that is hard to confuse with competitors.
Fix: Formalize entity signals with Organization schema specifying Traya's founding year, headquarters, and the specific hair loss mechanism the brand addresses. Add a brief brand description in schema that names the Ayurveda-plus-dermatology combination explicitly, as this is the specific differentiator AI engines should be extracting.
Extractable Structure68/100
Traya's website has more structured content than most Indian hair care brands with blog articles, hair test pathways, and condition-specific pages. The extractable structure weakens on product detail pages where ingredient rationales are described in marketing language rather than structured technical prose with H2 headers.
Fix: Add structured ingredient rationale sections to each product page using H2 subheadings: what this ingredient does, what the evidence base is, and how it interacts with other Traya products in the system. This transforms product pages into AI-extractable sources for ingredient queries.
Schema Markup62/100
Some schema implementation exists including review schema. FAQPage schema is inconsistently applied across the site. The hair test and condition-specific pages that are most relevant to AI engine queries for hair loss treatment lack structured markup that would make them primary citation sources.
Fix: Add FAQPage schema to the hair loss condition pages and the hair test landing page. Priority questions: how does the Traya personalized test work, is Traya effective for androgenetic alopecia, how long before Traya shows results, what does Traya contain and why. These are the questions buyers put into AI engines at the research stage.
FAQ Coverage65/100
Traya has better FAQ coverage than most hair care brands audited. The gap is in competitive comparison FAQs: buyers who ask AI engines whether Traya is better than Minoxidil, more effective than Arata, or comparable to dermatologist-prescribed treatments are not consistently routed to Traya-sourced answers.
Fix: Publish a comparison FAQ section: Traya versus Minoxidil for androgenetic alopecia, Traya versus Ayurvedic-only approaches, Traya versus biotin supplements for hair growth. Frame each comparison honestly based on what each approach targets and for which buyer profile each is most suitable. Add FAQPage schema.
Answer-Led Content65/100
Traya's blog and condition pages have reasonable answer-led content. Some articles still open with topic context rather than a direct answer. AI engines extract more readily from pages that answer the stated question in the first sentence.
Fix: Audit the 10 most visited Traya content pages and restructure any that do not open with a direct answer. For example, the hair loss causes article should open with: hair loss in Indian men and women is most commonly caused by androgenetic alopecia, accounting for approximately 80 percent of cases, followed by a direct statement of what Traya targets.
Specificity & Evidence60/100
Traya has some specificity evidence including dermatologist involvement and the personalized test model. Specific outcome data is limited: no published success rate percentage, no sample size for improvement claims, and no peer-reviewed citation for the combination approach efficacy. AI engines tend to surface brands with specific verifiable numbers more consistently.
Fix: Publish a results data page: what percentage of users reported reduced hair fall in 12 weeks based on internal data with sample size stated, what percentage reported new growth at 6 months, and how results compare between different hair loss types. State methodology clearly. This specificity would significantly improve AI citation for Traya outcome queries.

Frequently Asked Questions about Traya AI Visibility

Why is Traya not consistently recommended when buyers ask ChatGPT about hair loss India?
Traya scores 66 out of 100 on AI visibility and does surface in several tested hair loss queries. The gaps are in comparison queries, ingredient-specific queries, and outcome-evidence queries, where buyers ask AI engines to compare Traya with Minoxidil, explain what Traya contains, or cite specific success rate data. These question types are currently answered by third-party sources rather than structured Traya pages.
What is Traya and how does its approach to hair loss differ from standard treatments?
Traya is an Indian D2C hair care brand that offers a personalized hair loss treatment system combining Ayurvedic formulations, dermatological actives, and nutritional support. Buyers complete a hair test to receive a customized protocol. The approach targets hair loss through multiple pathways simultaneously rather than a single-ingredient treatment like Minoxidil or Finasteride.
Which competitors does ChatGPT recommend instead of Traya for hair fall India?
In our tested queries, Minoxidil-based treatments and dermatologist recommendations surfaced frequently for androgenetic alopecia queries. Minimalist surfaced for ingredient-specific hair care queries. Traya surfaced for personalized hair loss and Ayurvedic combination treatment queries. The brand most likely to be recommended instead of Traya depends on how the buyer frames the question to the AI engine.
How long does Traya take to show results for hair fall?
Traya's general guidance suggests visible improvement in hair fall reduction within 8 to 12 weeks of consistent use, with new hair growth evidence at 4 to 6 months. Specific published success rate data with sample size and methodology is not currently available in a structured, directly extractable format on traya.in, which may reduce how consistently AI engines can cite Traya for outcome-evidence queries.
Is Traya better than Minoxidil for hair loss?
Traya and Minoxidil target hair loss through different mechanisms and are not directly comparable as alternatives. Minoxidil is a topical vasodilator with a well-established evidence base for androgenetic alopecia. Traya's approach combines Ayurvedic herbs, dermatological actives, and nutrition to address hair loss through multiple pathways. Some Traya protocols include Minoxidil as one component. A dermatologist consultation is advisable for moderate to severe androgenetic alopecia before choosing either approach.
How does Traya's AI visibility compare to Arata and Minimalist?
In this audit, Minimalist scores 74 out of 100, Traya scores 66, and Arata scores 55. Minimalist's lead is driven by superior ingredient-level FAQ coverage and schema implementation. Traya's 66 reflects consistent surfacing for hair loss queries but gaps in comparison and evidence queries. Arata's 55 reflects a distinctive technology claim that is not yet structured for consistent AI extraction.
