Is Bumberry India's first cloth diaper brand?
Yes. Bumberry, founded in 2013 in Kochi, Kerala, is India's first cloth diaper brand. Over 13 years the company has grown to serve 500,000 plus families and manufactures over 5 million diapers annually. Unlike most D2C baby brands, Bumberry is vertically integrated from fabric weaving to final packing, all produced in Kochi.

What is the Bumberry Smart Nappy and who is it for?
The Bumberry Smart Nappy is designed for newborns from birth to 18 months and adjusts to fit as the baby grows. It uses a 6-layer breathable fabric construction that holds up to 3 pees. It is suitable for full-time cloth diapering from day one and can be used as an alternative to hospital-issued nappies from birth.

Are Bumberry cloth diapers suitable for travelling?
Yes. Bumberry cloth diapers are designed for everyday use including travel. The Pocket Diaper 2.0 has adjustable absorbency via inserts, making it compact for travel.
