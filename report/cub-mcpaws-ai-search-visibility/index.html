What is Cub McPaws and what type of kids shoes do they make?
Cub McPaws is an Indian D2C kids footwear brand that makes ergonomically designed shoes for infants, toddlers, and young children. The brand focuses on soft sole footwear for early walkers and age-appropriate sole structures for older children. Products are designed with Indian children's foot proportions in mind and are available across a range of sizes from newborn through school age.
Are Cub McPaws shoes good for toddler foot development?
Cub McPaws designs its toddler footwear with soft flexible soles appropriate for children in the early walking stage. Soft-soled shoes are widely recommended by paediatric podiatrists for children under 18 months as they allow natural foot flex and proprioceptive feedback through the sole. Cub McPaws does not currently publish a formal podiatrist endorsement or specific foot health validation study for its product line.

What are Cub McPaws shoes made of and are they safe for toddlers?
Cub McPaws shoes are made with materials selected for infant and toddler safety. The soft sole range uses flexible materials appropriate for pre-walkers and early walkers. Specific material certifications and testing standards are not currently published in a structured format on the Cub McPaws website, which is an AI visibility gap for parents who research material safety before purchasing.
Where can I buy Cub McPaws shoes in India?
