Extractable Structure 75/100
Age-based navigation (Newborn 0-9m, Toddler 9m-2y, Growing Up 2y plus) gives AI engines a useful structure for categorizing products by age. Category pages have product grids but limited structured text. Blog content exists and answers some questions but is inconsistently formatted for AI extraction.
Fix: Add a 200-300 word structured buying guide at the top of each category page with direct answers to the most common parent questions for that age group. Use H2 and H3 subheadings. This format is the most reliably extracted by large language models.
Schema Markup 68/100
Product schema and Review schema are present across product pages. BreadcrumbList is implemented. FAQPage schema is missing from category and home pages. HowTo schema for washing and care is absent despite multiple care guide articles existing on the blog.
Fix: Add FAQPage schema to home page and each age-group category page with 5-8 questions each. Retrofit HowTo schema onto existing washing guide articles. These are high-impact schema additions that directly increase AI citation frequency for how-to queries.
FAQ Coverage 58/100
A FAQ section exists on the site but covers basic purchase questions like shipping and returns. The deeper questions parents actually ask AI engines are not answered on SuperBottoms pages: cloth diaper versus disposable cost comparison, which diaper for a specific age and weight, nighttime diapering strategy, diaper rash prevention with cloth diapers.
Fix: Expand FAQ content to 40 plus questions organized by topic: sizing, care, cost comparison, nighttime use, travel use, cloth diaper for working parents. Publish these on a dedicated FAQ page and mark up with FAQPage schema. This directly captures the question-based queries AI engines receive.
Answer-Led Content 70/100
The SuperBottoms blog has well-written articles that answer common parent questions. Several rank in Google search. However, most blog posts open with brand storytelling or context rather than a direct answer to the question in the title. AI engines prefer content that opens with the direct answer in the first two sentences.
Fix: Audit the top 20 blog posts for answer-first structure. Move the direct answer to the opening paragraph of each post. This single structural change to existing content significantly improves AI citation rate because language models extract the first direct answer they find.
Specificity & Evidence 85/100
SuperBottoms has the strongest evidence base of all Indian cloth diaper brands: 20 lakh plus parents, voted best eco-friendly diaper for six consecutive years, CPSIA certified, celebrity endorsement. These are cited by AI engines when they appear in easily extractable text.
Fix: Create a dedicated numbers page listing all specific evidence: parent count, year founded, number of products, wash cycle durability data, environmental impact calculation, certification names and bodies. AI engines treat this as a reference page and cite from it frequently.
