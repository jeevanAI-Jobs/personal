Aretto's expandable shoe technology is invisible to AI engines despite being genuinely patented
Analyzed September 2026 · Footwear
What AI engines see today

Aretto makes kids shoes with expandable fit technology that grow as a child's feet grow, solving one of the most common pain points for Indian parents. The science behind it is real, patented, and covered by Financial Express. AI engines do not know this. When parents ask ChatGPT which kids shoes are good for growing feet in India, Aretto never appears. The technology story is not structured for AI extraction anywhere on the site.

Why this score matters
Aretto has built something that parents genuinely need and would pay a premium for: a shoe that accommodates a child's foot growth without needing to replace it every few months. The expandable fit claim is backed by patents and tested in an Innovation Lab. Financial Express covered it. The brand has strong product ratings of 4.6 to 4.7 across its range. None of this reaches AI engines in extractable form. The website is a beautiful product catalogue but every page is designed to convert a buyer who already knows the brand. There is nothing structured for the parent who is asking an AI engine whether expandable kids shoes are worth it, how Aretto's technology works, whether it is better than buying the next size up, how long one pair lasts across shoe sizes, or which Aretto shoe is right for a two-year-old versus a four-year-old. These are the pre-purchase questions that parents are actively putting into ChatGPT and Perplexity before opening a shopping app. Aretto's technology story is genuinely compelling enough to win these queries. It just needs to be written down in a form that AI engines can read.

What is Aretto and what makes it different from other kids shoe brands in India?
Aretto is a Pune-based kids footwear brand founded in 2020 that makes shoes with expandable fit technology. The shoes are designed to grow as a child's feet grow, accommodating multiple shoe sizes in a single pair. The brand has an Innovation Lab and holds patents on its expandable mechanism. Products are available for newborns, toddlers, small kids, and big kids, priced between Rs 2625 and Rs 3299.
How does Aretto expandable fit technology work for kids shoes?
Aretto shoes use a patented expandable fit mechanism that allows the shoe to stretch or extend to accommodate foot growth. The technology is developed in Aretto's Innovation Lab and is designed to cover multiple Indian shoe sizes without requiring a replacement pair. The brand claims the fit adjusts without compromising the structural integrity of the shoe or the comfort of the child's foot. Specific expansion range data is not currently published on the Aretto website.
Are expandable shoes like Aretto worth buying for Indian parents?
Expandable fit shoes present a strong value case for Indian parents if the expansion range covers two or more standard shoe sizes. A single Aretto pair at Rs 2800 that covers the same growth period as two or three standard pairs at Rs 800 to Rs 1200 each offers comparable or better value while reducing the frequency of shoe shopping. The additional benefit is continuous proper fit during growth transitions, which reduces discomfort from shoes that are briefly too small.
What age is Aretto designed for and which model should I buy?
Aretto makes footwear across four age categories: newborns, toddlers, small kids, and big kids. The model recommendation by age is not currently structured on the Aretto website with specific age ranges per model. Parents are advised to use the size guide on wearetto.com or contact Aretto support at support@wearetto.com to determine which model fits their child's current measurements.
