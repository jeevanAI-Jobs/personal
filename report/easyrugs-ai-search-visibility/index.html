What AI engines see today

EasyRugs is positioned as India's first washable rug brand, selling hand washable, pet-friendly, anti-skid rugs in a Jaipur-New York design aesthetic. The brand was founded by Harnaam Kaur and has received media coverage. In tested queries for washable rugs India and machine washable rug India, EasyRugs surfaced in some variations. The brand's 500 plus five-star reviews and the water-resistant and stain-resistant fabric innovation are strong signals, but structured FAQ content answering how washing works, what sizes are available, and how EasyRugs compares to standard rugs on durability is not present in an AI-extractable format on the website.

Why this score matters
EasyRugs has identified a genuine consumer pain point in the Indian market: standard rugs are difficult to clean, yet rugs are a high-foot-traffic, spill-prone home item. The washable rug concept is well-established internationally but EasyRugs is among the first to position around it specifically for Indian consumers. The AI visibility challenge is that buyers who research rug purchases on AI engines typically ask practical questions: how to choose the right rug size for a living room, what material is best for rugs in Indian climate conditions, how often rugs should be cleaned, and whether machine washing damages rug fibres. EasyRugs has the product expertise to answer all of these questions with a washable-rug angle, but none of these answers currently exist on the website in a structured FAQ format that AI engines extract from.

Can I machine wash a rug at home India ChatGPT answer instead of EasyRugs
In tested queries for how to wash a rug at home India and machine washable rug India, answers came from general home cleaning guides and international rug brands rather than EasyRugs pages. This is the highest-intent query for EasyRugs' core buyer: someone who has a rug that needs cleaning and is wondering whether machine washing is possible. EasyRugs' entire positioning is built around answering yes to this question, but the structured answer does not exist on easyrugs.in in a format AI engines extract.
What to do: Publish a dedicated washing guide page titled how to wash your EasyRug complete care guide. Cover: which EasyRug products can be hand washed, whether any can be put in a washing machine and at what temperature setting, what detergent to use, how to dry without shrinkage or colour fade, how often to wash for different room types (pet homes, children's rooms, entryways), and what happens to the anti-skid backing with repeated washing. Add HowTo schema. This single page would intercept the highest-volume washable rug query and directly convert a buyer with a cleaning concern.
What size rug for living room India standard dimensions guide ChatGPT
Rug sizing is the most common pre-purchase research question across all rug categories. Buyers who ask AI engines what size rug to buy for a 12 by 14 room receive general home decor guidance. EasyRugs has the opportunity to own the Indian living room sizing answer specifically because Indian living room dimensions and furniture configurations differ from Western standards, and there is no widely cited India-specific rug sizing guide for AI engines to reference.
What to do: Publish a rug size guide specific to Indian homes: standard rug sizes for a 2BHK and 3BHK living room, rug size recommendations for Indian dining areas with standard dining table dimensions, bedroom rug placement guide for queen and king bed sizes common in India, and entryway rug sizes for Indian apartment doors and corridors. Use a structured table format with H2 subheadings per room type. Add FAQPage schema with 8 sizing questions. This page would surface EasyRugs for every rug sizing query from Indian buyers.
Best rug for homes with pets India washable and non-shedding on AI engines
Pet owners in India searching for rugs represent a high-intent, frustrated buyer segment: standard rugs trap pet hair, absorb pet odours, and cannot be easily washed. In tested queries for pet-friendly rugs India and washable rug for dog India, EasyRugs surfaced in some variations but was not the consistent specific recommendation. A dedicated pet-owner rug guide would directly intercept this segment.
What to do: Publish a complete guide for pet owners buying rugs in India: why standard rugs are problematic for pet homes (odour, hair, accidents), what the specific features of a pet-friendly rug should be (washable, low pile, stain-resistant, colour patterns that camouflage hair), how to wash a rug after a pet accident, and which EasyRugs designs and materials are best suited to pet homes. Add FAQPage schema with 8 pet-related questions. This guide would rank for every pet-owner rug query and make EasyRugs the specific recommendation for this large and active buyer segment.

What is EasyRugs and what makes its rugs different from standard rugs?
EasyRugs is an Indian rug brand positioned as India's first washable rug brand, founded by Harnaam Kaur. The rugs are made from water-resistant and stain-resistant fabrics that allow hand washing at home, unlike standard rugs which require professional dry cleaning or are damaged by water. EasyRugs are described as hand washable, sustainable, pet-friendly, and anti-skid. The brand's Jaipur-New York collection applies Jaipur craft heritage to contemporary design aesthetics. Prices start from approximately Rs 3,118 and the brand has received 500 plus five-star reviews.
Can EasyRugs be machine washed or only hand washed?
EasyRugs are described as hand washable on their website. The specific washing instructions for each product, including whether any products are machine-wash compatible and at what temperature setting, are available on individual product pages at easyrugs.in. The brand's positioning around washability specifically addresses the Indian buyer's need to clean rugs at home without professional cleaning services.
Are EasyRugs suitable for homes with pets and children?
EasyRugs markets its products as pet-friendly and describes the anti-skid backing as a safety feature relevant for active households.
