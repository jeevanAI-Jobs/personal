What AI engines see today

Snitch is the most AI-visible D2C clothing brand in India. Its Shark Tank India Season 2 backstory, Rs 520 crore FY25 revenue, and high social media presence give AI engines strong signals to cite it. The gap is in styling advisory and buyer guidance content: which Snitch pieces for which occasions, how to size, what to expect on quality.

Why this score matters
Snitch has done more than almost any Indian D2C clothing brand to build AI visibility without specifically trying to. The Shark Tank India Season 2 story is widely covered. Revenue milestones make business news. The brand's daily new drops model creates constant social media content. All of this feeds AI entity strength. When someone asks ChatGPT which Indian men's fashion brand is worth buying from, Snitch almost always appears. The gap is in buyer advisory content. Snitch's site has a strong product catalogue but limited structured content answering the specific questions buyers ask before ordering: how does the sizing run, which fabrics are best for summer versus winter, how does Snitch quality hold up after washing, which pieces are versatile enough for both office and casual wear. These are the queries that convert browsers to buyers, and they are currently being answered by third-party fashion blogs rather than Snitch's own site.

Content Gaps Costing AI Citations

3 gaps found
Snitch sizing guide for Indian men height and weight
Sizing is the single biggest barrier to first purchase for Indian D2C clothing brands. AI engines receive this query constantly and route buyers to third-party reviews instead of Snitch's own size guide because the current guide is standard and not structured for AI extraction.
What to do: Publish a detailed sizing guide structured as a FAQ: what size should a 5 foot 8 Indian man with 38 inch chest order from Snitch, how does Snitch large compare to other brands. Add FAQPage schema. This page would capture all Snitch sizing queries from AI engines.
Snitch quality review fabric wash durability
Quality queries are the second biggest barrier for new Snitch buyers. AI engines currently cite third-party review sites for Snitch quality questions. The brand should control this narrative with its own specific data.
What to do: Publish a Snitch fabric quality page with specific data: cotton GSM by product type, shrinkage after 30 washes, color retention test results, and customer testimonials focused on longevity. Add FAQPage schema with 8 wash and quality questions.
Snitch versus Bewakoof versus H and M quality price comparison India
Brand comparison queries are extremely high intent. Buyers who ask this question are ready to purchase. Whichever brand page answers the comparison most honestly and specifically gets the conversion.
What to do: Publish a comparison guide: Snitch versus fast fashion on price per wear, fabric quality, and style differentiation. Frame Snitch's premium positioning honestly. AI engines will cite this page every time buyers ask which D2C clothing brand is worth buying in India.

Fix: Formalize the entity with Organization schema listing all key facts: founding year, founder Siddharth Dungarwal, Bengaluru HQ, Shark Tank Season 2 deal, FY25 revenue Rs 520 crore. This makes AI engine citations more accurate and more specific.
Extractable Structure68/100
Product pages are well structured with size guides, fabric information, and styling suggestions. Category pages have solid navigation. The gap is at the content layer: no editorial buying guides, no structured comparison content, no FAQ sections on category pages.
Fix: Add a 200-word editorial section to each major category page answering the buyer question most relevant to that category. For shirts: how to choose the right Snitch shirt for office vs casual. For jeans: how Snitch sizing runs compared to standard Indian sizing.
Schema Markup68/100
Product schema and Review schema are well implemented. BreadcrumbList is present. FAQPage schema is absent sitewide. HowTo schema for styling guides is missing despite Snitch having the content to support it.
Fix: Add FAQPage schema to the homepage and each major category landing page. Priority questions: how does Snitch sizing run, is Snitch fabric good quality, how often does Snitch drop new styles, what is the return policy. These are the specific questions buyers ask AI engines before ordering.
FAQ Coverage55/100
A basic FAQ page exists covering policies. Buyer advisory FAQs are absent: questions about fabric quality, wash durability, styling by occasion, and size comparison to other Indian brands are all unanswered in structured FAQ format.
Fix: Expand FAQ coverage to include 25 buyer advisory questions across five topics: Sizing and Fit, Fabric and Quality, Styling by Occasion, New Drops and How to Shop, and Sustainability. Mark up every question with FAQPage schema.
Answer-Led Content65/100

Each article must answer the question in the first sentence.

Fix: Create a Snitch quality page with specific product evidence: fabric weight by category, wash test results, shrinkage data, and a sizing comparison table showing how Snitch sizes map to standard Indian sizing charts. AI engines cite these specifics when buyers ask quality and sizing questions.

Frequently Asked Questions about Snitch AI Visibility

Is Snitch the most AI-visible clothing brand in India?
Snitch scores 69 out of 100 on AI visibility, making it the highest-scoring D2C clothing brand in this analysis. Its Shark Tank India Season 2 deal, Rs 520 crore FY25 revenue, and consistent media coverage give AI engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity strong signals to cite it for men's fashion queries in India.
Did Snitch get a deal on Shark Tank India?
Yes. Snitch appeared on Shark Tank India Season 2 Episode 13 and secured a deal of Rs 1.5 crore for 1.5 percent equity from Aman Gupta of boAt. The brand was founded by Siddharth Dungarwal in Bengaluru and has grown to Rs 520 crore in revenue by FY25, making it one of the most successful Shark Tank India alumni.
How does Snitch sizing run for Indian men?
Snitch uses standard Indian sizing but the fit tends to run slightly slim. Indian men with broader shoulders or a larger chest typically size up by one from their usual size. For example, someone who usually wears a medium in mainstream brands may prefer a large in Snitch for a comfortable fit. The brand offers free exchanges, which makes it easier to find the right size on the first order.
Is Snitch quality worth the price compared to Bewakoof or H and M?
Snitch positions itself between fast fashion and premium basics. Fabric quality is generally above Bewakoof but below premium brands. For buyers who want trendy styles with acceptable quality at mid-range prices, Snitch delivers strong value. The brand's daily new drop model prioritizes trend speed over fabric longevity, so pieces are designed more for seasonal style than multi-year durability.
What AI visibility gaps does Snitch have despite its revenue and Shark Tank fame?
Snitch's main AI gaps are in buyer advisory content: sizing guides structured for AI extraction, fabric quality FAQs with specific data, and styling guides for different occasions. When buyers ask AI engines specific questions like how does Snitch sizing run or is Snitch quality good after washing, answers come from third-party review blogs rather than Snitch's own site.
Does Snitch appear in Google AI Mode and Perplexity for clothing brand recommendations?
Yes. Snitch appears consistently in Perplexity and Google AI Mode for D2C menswear and clothing brand queries in India. For broader queries like best Indian clothing brand for casual wear, Snitch typically appears in the top three citations. For buyer advisory queries about sizing or quality, citations are less consistent because the site lacks FAQ schema.
