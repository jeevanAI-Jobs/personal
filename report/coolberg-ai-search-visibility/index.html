Coolberg leads non-alcoholic beer AI citations but loses buyer advisory queries
Analyzed September 2026 · Beverages
What AI engines see today

As India's first non-alcoholic beer brand, Coolberg has strong entity clarity and appears in most AI engine answers for non-alcoholic beer queries. The gap is in buyer advisory content: comparison, occasion guides, and FAQ schema that would make Coolberg the dominant AI citation for the category rather than just one of several mentions.

Why this score matters
Coolberg is the default answer AI engines give when anyone asks about non-alcoholic beer in India, and that is a genuine competitive advantage earned over years in the market. The brand is pioneer-positioned and AI engines respect longevity signals. The problem is that being mentioned is different from being recommended. When buyers ask follow-up questions, is Coolberg right for me, which flavor should I try first, how does it taste compared to real beer, AI engines often route to third-party food blogs and magazine articles rather than Coolberg's own website. This happens because Coolberg's site is built for ordering, not for answering. There is no FAQ section, no comparison guide, no occasion-specific content. The opportunity cost is significant: Coolberg is already winning the brand query, but losing the buyer decision query to content it does not own.

What flavors does Coolberg offer?
Coolberg offers six flavors of non-alcoholic beer: Cranberry, Strawberry, Ginger, Peach, Mint, and Malt. The Malt variant is the most traditional tasting and closest to a lager profile. The fruit variants like Strawberry and Cranberry appeal to buyers who want a fruit-forward non-alcoholic option. All variants contain 0 percent alcohol.
Is Coolberg non-alcoholic beer suitable for Muslims and pregnant women?
Coolberg contains 0 percent alcohol, making it suitable for consumers who avoid alcohol for religious reasons including Muslims observing halal dietary guidelines. For pregnant women, Coolberg is generally considered safe as a non-alcoholic beverage, but as with any dietary choice during pregnancy, consulting a doctor is recommended. The product is also suitable for teetotallers, teenagers, and drivers.
Where is Coolberg available in India?
Coolberg is available across India through modern retail chains, premium supermarkets, and online grocery platforms. The brand has strong distribution in metro cities and is available in bars and restaurants that want to offer a non-alcoholic beer option. For online purchase, Coolberg is listed on major e-commerce platforms. Availability varies by city and pin code.
