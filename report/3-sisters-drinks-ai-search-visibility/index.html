Extractable Structure38/100
The website is primarily a product catalogue with flavor grids and buy buttons. AI engines may have difficulty extracting conversational answers from product catalogue pages. There is no text content explaining why non-alcoholic beer, what occasions it suits, or how 3 Sisters differs from a standard mocktail.
Fix: Add a 300-word structured explainer section to the homepage and each product category page answering the core question for that format: what is non-alcoholic beer and why choose it. Use H2 and H3 subheadings throughout so AI engines can parse the content hierarchy.
Schema Markup35/100
Product schema is minimal. FAQPage schema is completely absent sitewide. HowTo schema for mocktail recipes or cocktail mixing guides is missing. Organization schema is not implemented. BreadcrumbList is absent. These are the exact schema types that drive AI engine citations.
Fix: Prioritize adding FAQPage schema to the homepage with 8 to 10 questions covering what is non-alcoholic beer, is it suitable for Muslims and pregnant women, what does Super Cola taste like, and how is No Bullshit different from Red Bull. Each FAQ schema entry directly increases AI citation probability.
FAQ Coverage28/100

People ask AI engines very specific questions before buying a non-alcoholic drink: is it really 0 percent alcohol, does it taste like real beer, can teenagers drink it, is it available on Zepto. None of these questions are answered in structured content.
Fix: Publish a 30-question FAQ page covering five topic areas: What is non-alcoholic beer, Is it safe for children pregnant women and Muslims, How does it compare to regular beer, Where to buy in India, and Occasions and uses. Mark every question with FAQPage schema immediately.

Each article must open with a direct answer to the question in the title.

Does 3 Sisters Drinks appear on ChatGPT recommendations for non-alcoholic drinks in India?

Did 3 Sisters Drinks get a deal on Shark Tank India?
3 Sisters Drinks appeared on Shark Tank India Season 5 Episode 30 and asked for 3 crore rupees for 2.5 percent equity at a 120 crore valuation. They did not receive any deal. Shark Vineeta Singh described the brand identity as a distraction and Shark Kunal Bahl noted the strong team but lack of focus. The founders continued building the brand after the show.
What products does 3 Sisters Drinks make?
3 Sisters Drinks makes six product lines: non-alcoholic beers in 7 flavors including German Lager, Ginger, Peach, Strawberry, Cranberry, Kiwi Mint and Peach Mango; sugar-free non-alcoholic beers; Indie-Soda in Indian flavors like Twisted Shikanji and Kala Khatta; Aruba Mocktails and Mixers in 7 variants; Super Cola made with real dates and zero sugar; and No Bullshit, a caffeinated energy drink with 75mg caffeine.
How does 3 Sisters Drinks compare to Coolberg for non-alcoholic beer in India?
Coolberg is India's oldest non-alcoholic beer brand and scores 64 on AI visibility versus 3 Sisters' 42. Coolberg has stronger entity clarity and content depth built over more years. 3 Sisters has wider distribution with 7000 plus outlets versus Coolberg's primarily premium channel presence. On product variety 3 Sisters offers more formats including mocktails, energy drinks and Indian-flavored sodas that Coolberg does not.
Is 3 Sisters non-alcoholic beer suitable for Muslims and pregnant women?
3 Sisters Drinks products contain 0 percent alcohol. The non-alcoholic beers, mocktails and Indie-Soda range are suitable for consumers who avoid alcohol for religious, health or personal reasons. The No Bullshit energy drink contains caffeine at 75mg per can, so pregnant women should check with their doctor before consuming it. All other products in the range are caffeine-free.
Where can I buy 3 Sisters Drinks in India?
3 Sisters Drinks is available at 7000 plus retail locations across 25 Indian states and union territories. Retail partners include 7-Eleven, Spencer's, Miraj Cinema, and Wow! Momo. For online delivery, the products are listed on Zepto, Flipkart, and BigBasket. The brand also serves 10 plus international locations.
