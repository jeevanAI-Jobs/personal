Entity Clarity58/100
Brand name 'A Toddler Thing' reads as generic to AI entity classifiers. No dedicated about page linking the brand to its Shark Tank India appearance, founder identity, or Aikya Clothing Pvt. Ltd company registration.
Fix: Create a dedicated brand story page with Organization schema naming the brand, founder, founding year, and Shark Tank Season 3 investment. This gives AI engines a canonical entity reference to cite.
Extractable Structure45/100
Category pages are product grid layouts. AI engines may have difficulty extracting direct answers from grid pages because there are no headers, subheadings, or structured text blocks. Content is largely absent from our tested queries to language model retrieval.
Fix: Add a buying guide section to each category page with H2 subheadings, bulleted comparisons, and direct answers to common parent questions. Even 300 words of structured text per category page would significantly improve extractability.
Schema Markup52/100
Product schema and basic Organization schema are present. FAQPage schema is completely absent. HowTo schema for cloth diaper care is missing. BreadcrumbList is not implemented sitewide.
Fix: Add FAQPage schema to home page and all category pages. Add HowTo schema to any care or washing guide content. Each FAQ schema item directly increases the chance of appearing in AI-generated answers.
FAQ Coverage32/100
No dedicated FAQ page or FAQ section exists on the site. Parents ask AI engines dozens of specific questions about cloth diapers, muslin care, organic certifications, and toddler sizing. None of these have direct answers on the A Toddler Thing site.
Fix: Publish a 25-question FAQ page targeting the exact questions parents ask AI engines. Topics should include cloth diaper fit by weight, washing muslin at home, organic cotton vs conventional, and Shark Tank India story. Mark it up with FAQPage schema.
Answer-Led Content38/100
Every page is written to sell, not to answer. Product descriptions lead with benefits and CTAs. AI systems can more easily extract content that opens with a direct answer to a question, followed by supporting detail. The current site structure produces zero pages that match this format.
Fix: Rewrite at least 5 category pages to open with a direct answer to a parent question. Example: 'Which cloth diaper fits a 6kg newborn? The A Toddler Thing newborn diaper fits 3kg to 9kg and adjusts via snap buttons.' This one change makes the page AI-extractable.
Specificity & Evidence65/100
The site mentions '1,00,000+ parents trust ATT' and '4.7 stars' but does not provide certification names, third-party lab test results, or specific cost-per-wash figures that AI systems find it easier to process to cite as factual evidence.
Fix: Add a Trust and Certifications page listing the exact organic certification names, dye standards, test labs used, and cost-per-wash calculation with real numbers. AI engines cite specific numbers far more than general claims.
