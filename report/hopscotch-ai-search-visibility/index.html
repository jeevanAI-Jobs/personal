Why this score matters
Hopscotch has built one of the strongest entity signals of any Indian kids D2C brand. Years of operation, a large product catalogue, and consistent media coverage mean it tends to surface more consistently in tested queries for kids fashion queries. The footwear category is where Hopscotch shows its structural weakness: it is a fashion-led platform, not a footwear specialist. The opportunity is to publish footwear-specific content that captures the health-research queries and converts that parent into a buyer.

Specific return terms are listed on the Hopscotch website and may vary by product type and seller.
