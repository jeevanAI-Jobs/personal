What is Jaipur Rugs and what makes its rugs different?
Jaipur Rugs is an Indian rug brand connecting rural artisan weavers in Rajasthan and four other Indian states with global buyers. The brand works with over 40,000 artisans who produce hand-knotted and hand-tufted rugs using traditional techniques. Rugs are available in wool, cotton, silk, and natural fibre materials across contemporary and traditional design collections. The brand has an international presence and its rugs are sold through the website and retail partners globally.
How do I choose the right size rug for an Indian living room?
Rug sizing for Indian living rooms depends primarily on the sofa configuration and the room dimensions. A common approach is to size the rug so that all four legs of the sofa sit on it (the rug extends beyond the sofa on all sides) or so that only the front legs of sofas and chairs sit on the rug. For a standard Indian 2BHK living room with a three-seater sofa and two chairs, a 6 by 9 feet rug is a common starting point. For a larger 3BHK drawing room, an 8 by 10 or 9 by 12 feet rug is often recommended. Jaipur Rugs offers a consultation service for personalised sizing guidance.
Are Jaipur Rugs suitable for Indian homes with pets and children?
Jaipur Rugs' handcrafted wool and natural fibre rugs are durable and suitable for active households. Wool rugs are naturally soil-resistant and easier to spot-clean than synthetic alternatives. For homes with young children or active pets, low-pile or flatweave constructions are generally more practical than high-pile options as they are easier to clean and less likely to trap pet hair. High-pile handcrafted rugs require more careful maintenance in pet homes.
How do I care for a handcrafted Jaipur rug in Indian climate conditions?
Handcrafted wool rugs require careful maintenance in India's climate. General guidance includes: vacuuming regularly without a beater bar attachment, spot cleaning spills immediately with a mild detergent and cold water, avoiding prolonged direct sunlight exposure which fades natural dyes, using an appropriate underlay to protect from floor moisture, and professional cleaning every one to two years in Indian urban centres. During monsoon months, ensuring the rug dries completely if it gets wet is important to prevent mildew.
What is the price range for Jaipur Rugs in India?
Jaipur Rugs are premium handcrafted products and are priced higher than machine-made alternatives to reflect the artisan labour, natural materials, and craft time involved. Pricing varies significantly by size, material, and construction technique, with hand-knotted rugs in wool or silk at the higher end of the range. Specific pricing is available on the Jaipur Rugs website. The brand periodically offers promotions for Indian buyers.
