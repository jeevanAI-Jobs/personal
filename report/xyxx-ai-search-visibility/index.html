What is XYXX and what makes its innerwear different?
XYXX is an Indian premium innerwear brand focused on men's underwear made from Tencel and micro-modal fabrics. The brand is positioned around fabric performance: softer, more breathable, and more moisture-wicking than standard cotton. XYXX products are available on its website and through Indian e-commerce platforms.
Is Tencel fabric good for Indian conditions?
Tencel (branded as Lyocell by Lenzing) is generally considered well-suited to Indian conditions. It is significantly more breathable and moisture-wicking than standard cotton, which helps with sweat management in India's heat and humidity. It is also smooth on the skin, reducing irritation in high-contact areas. Tencel is produced from wood pulp using a closed-loop process, making it more sustainable than conventional cotton. These properties make it relevant for daily wear in Indian conditions.
How do I choose the right size in XYXX underwear?
XYXX uses standard S, M, L, XL, and XXL sizing. The brand recommends referring to the size guide on its website, which uses waist measurement in inches as the primary sizing reference. XYXX's Tencel and modal fabrics have natural stretch, so the fit tends to be comfortable across a range within each size. Buyers who are between sizes generally size down for a snug fit or size up for a relaxed fit.
Is XYXX innerwear suitable for gym and active use?
XYXX's Tencel and micro-modal products are suitable for moderate active use including gym sessions. The fabric's moisture-wicking and breathable properties help manage sweat. For high-intensity or long-duration athletic use, dedicated athletic underwear with compression support may be more appropriate. XYXX is well-suited to buyers who want active-capable innerwear that also works for everyday office and casual wear.
How do I care for XYXX Tencel underwear to make it last longer?
Tencel and micro-modal fabrics last longest when washed in cold or warm water (not hot), using a gentle or delicate cycle, and air-dried rather than machine-dried. Tumble drying on high heat degrades the fibre structure over time. Avoid bleach and fabric softener, which break down the fibre coating. Following these instructions, Tencel innerwear should last significantly longer than standard cotton with the same washing frequency.
