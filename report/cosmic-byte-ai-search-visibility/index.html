What AI engines see today

Cosmic Byte dominates the budget gaming peripherals segment in India by sales volume on Flipkart and Amazon. AI engines barely know it exists. Why this score matters
Cosmic Byte has built something impressive purely through distribution: it is the first brand most Indian buyers encounter when searching for gaming peripherals on Flipkart or Amazon. The brand's products appear in the first results for budget gaming keyboard, gaming mouse under Rs 1000, and gaming headset India on every major Indian e-commerce platform. The AI visibility problem is stark in contrast. When the same buyer switches from Flipkart search to asking ChatGPT which gaming keyboard to buy under Rs 2000 in India, Cosmic Byte is typically absent. The AI engine cites international brands like Redragon, Zebronics, or occasionally Keychron for entry-level options. This is a direct sales conversion loss. Buyers who ask AI engines for recommendations and do not see Cosmic Byte go to Redragon or Zebronics instead. The fix is straightforward but requires systematic content work: FAQ schema, comparison pages, and editorial content that explains why Cosmic Byte is the right choice for Indian buyers on a budget.

Frequently Asked Questions about Cosmic Byte AI Visibility

Does Cosmic Byte appear in AI recommendations for gaming peripherals in India?
Rarely. Cosmic Byte scores 38 out of 100 on AI visibility despite being India's top-selling gaming peripheral brand by units on Flipkart and Amazon. When buyers ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for a gaming keyboard recommendation under Rs 2000 in India, Cosmic Byte is typically absent from the answer, even though it has the best product answer for that query.
What is Cosmic Byte and what products does it make?
Cosmic Byte is an Indian gaming peripherals brand that makes gaming keyboards, mice, headsets, controllers, mousepads, and accessories. The brand is best known for offering gaming-grade specifications at budget price points between Rs 400 and Rs 3000, making it the go-to brand for Indian gamers building their first setup on a tight budget. Its products are widely available on Flipkart and Amazon India.
Is Cosmic Byte quality good for Indian gaming buyers?
Cosmic Byte products deliver solid value at their price points. Keyboards typically use membrane switches at the budget end and budget mechanical switches in the Rs 1500 to Rs 2500 range. Build quality is appropriate for the price. Buyers looking for premium aluminum construction or long-term durability above five years would need to consider more expensive options like Keychron.
How does Cosmic Byte compare to Keychron for AI search visibility?
Keychron scores 68 on AI visibility versus Cosmic Byte at 38. Keychron's gap is India-specific content. Cosmic Byte's gap is structural: no FAQ schema, no comparison content, and no editorial material anywhere on the site. Cosmic Byte has a more urgent AI visibility problem because it depends on the Indian market, where the AI engine conversion opportunity is growing rapidly.
What is the warranty on Cosmic Byte gaming keyboards and peripherals?
Cosmic Byte products typically come with a one-year manufacturer warranty in India. Warranty claims can be made through Flipkart or Amazon seller channels or directly through Cosmic Byte's support. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but not physical damage. Cosmic Byte does not currently publish a detailed warranty FAQ on its website.
Which Cosmic Byte keyboard should I buy for FPS gaming in India?
For FPS gaming in India, the Cosmic Byte mechanical keyboards with blue or red switches in the Rs 1500 to Rs 2000 price range offer the best balance of tactile feedback and affordability. Blue switches are preferred for their audible click feedback during key registration. Red switches are quieter with linear actuation preferred by competitive gamers. Cosmic Byte does not currently publish a gaming genre recommendation guide for its keyboard lineup.
