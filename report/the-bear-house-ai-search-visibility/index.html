Why this score matters
The Bear House has done something genuinely difficult in Indian D2C fashion: established a premium positioning for everyday men's basics without celebrity backing or a massive marketing budget. The brand has a loyal customer base and clear product identity. The AI visibility problem is a structural one. Premium brand positioning requires a lot of justification content for AI engines to cite it confidently. When a buyer asks why The Bear House is worth the premium over a standard brand, AI engines need a page on The Bear House website that answers this question clearly and specifically. That page does not exist. Neither does a fabric quality page, a sizing guide structured for AI extraction, or a comparison against mainstream brands. The brand's story is being told by fashion reviewers and bloggers rather than by The Bear House itself, which means AI engines cite those third parties as the source of truth for the brand rather than thebearhouse.in.
