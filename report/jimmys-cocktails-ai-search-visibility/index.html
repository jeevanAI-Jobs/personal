What AI engines see today

Jimmy's Cocktails has strong brand recognition in the premium non-alcoholic mixer category and good content volume in its niche. The gap is category expansion: as the brand has extended into non-alcoholic beers, AI engines still associate it narrowly with mixers, missing significant buyer intent for its full product range.

Why this score matters
Jimmy's Cocktails built genuine brand authority in the premium non-alcoholic mixer segment. When someone asks an AI engine for a good mocktail mixer in India, Jimmy's appears consistently. That is a real asset. The challenge is that the brand has expanded its product range into non-alcoholic beers and sparkling beverages, but AI engines have not updated their mental model of what Jimmy's is. This is a classic entity expansion problem. The original brand signal was so strong for mixers that the new product lines do not get attributed to Jimmy's in AI answers. The fix is dedicated product expansion content that explicitly links the Jimmy's Cocktails brand entity to each new category, with supporting FAQ schema and structured comparison content that makes the expanded range visible to AI engines.
