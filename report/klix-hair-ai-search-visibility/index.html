What is Klix Hair and what products does it offer?
Klix Hair is an Indian D2C brand specializing in scalp toppers and clip-in hair extensions for women with thinning hair or hair loss. Products include scalp toppers in different base sizes and materials, clip-in extensions in various lengths and colors, and related hair accessories. The brand targets women experiencing diffuse thinning, postpartum hair loss, and alopecia, positioning scalp toppers as a non-surgical coverage solution.
How does Klix Hair compare to HairOriginals for hair extensions in India?
Klix Hair focuses on scalp toppers and extensions specifically for women with hair loss, with a product range oriented toward that concern. HairOriginals has a broader product range including scalp toppers, full wigs, men's patches, and volumizers, with the additional service infrastructure of 12 experience centers and a Try At Home consultation service. HairOriginals scored 47 out of 100 and Klix Hair scored 54 out of 100 on AI visibility in this audit.
Can scalp toppers from Klix Hair be used for alopecia areata in India?
Scalp toppers are commonly used for diffuse thinning and crown or part-line coverage. For alopecia areata, which causes patchy hair loss, the suitability of a scalp topper depends on the size and location of the patch relative to the topper's base dimensions. Full wigs may be more appropriate for larger or multiple patches. A consultation with a hair specialist is recommended before selecting a product. Klix Hair does not currently publish structured guidance specifically for alopecia areata buyers in an AI-extractable format.
How long do Klix Hair scalp toppers last with daily use?
The longevity of a scalp topper depends on the base material, the attachment method, and daily maintenance practices. General guidance for human hair scalp toppers suggests 1 to 2 years of daily use with proper care: gentle washing with sulphate-free products, air drying where possible, and proper storage. Klix Hair does not currently publish a specific durability claim with sample size or care protocol in an easily verifiable format on its website.

For current pricing, the Klix Hair website is the most reliable source.
