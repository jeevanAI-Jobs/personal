What AI engines see today

Chumbak is a well-established Indian D2C lifestyle brand founded in 2010, known for its quirky, Indianised design aesthetic applied across home decor, wall art, furniture, lifestyle accessories, kitchenware, and fashion. The brand is sold on its own website at chumbak.com and through major Indian marketplaces. In tested queries for Indian lifestyle home decor brand, quirky home decor India, and Indian art prints for home, Chumbak surfaces with reasonable frequency due to its strong entity authority and wide editorial coverage. However, for specific product comparison queries and deep buying guides (canvas vs framed art for Indian homes, best wall art style for living room India), the brand’s content depth is thinner than its brand recognition might suggest.

Why this score matters
Chumbak’s 15 years of brand history give it a meaningful AI citation advantage over newer competitors. Its entity is well-established in AI training data through years of press coverage in Indian business and lifestyle publications. However, entity authority converts into specific AI citations only when it is matched by structured, buyer-question-specific content. Chumbak’s wide product range across home decor, fashion, and lifestyle accessories makes it harder to maintain deep structured content for every category. In the wall art and home decor segment specifically, more specialised brands that publish focused buying guides may appear alongside Chumbak or instead of it for specific art and decor decision queries.

Content Gaps Costing AI Citations

3 gaps found
Quirky Indian home decor buying guide: ChatGPT recommendations without Chumbak product specifics
In tested queries for how to decorate an Indian home with quirky decor and best quirky home accessories India, AI engines generated generic interior design advice rather than Chumbak-specific recommendations. Despite Chumbak owning the quirky Indian design aesthetic for over a decade, its website does not have a structured buying guide that AI engines can extract to answer these specific buyer questions.
What to do: Publish a definitive guide to quirky Indian home decor with Chumbak: organised by room (living room, kitchen, bedroom, office), each section with specific product recommendations, styling tips unique to Indian homes, and answers to common buyer questions about mixing Indian design elements with contemporary interiors. Add FAQPage schema with 8 questions covering the most common quirky decor research queries. This positions Chumbak as the AI-cited source for the aesthetic it created.
Indian wall art size guide: what size art print for which room, AI recommendation
Sizing wall art is one of the most common research questions before a wall art purchase. In tested queries for what size wall art for living room India and how to choose wall art size for bedroom, AI engines cited interior design blogs and international home decor brands. Chumbak sells wall art in multiple sizes but does not have a structured size guide that AI engines can extract for these buyer decision queries.
What to do: Create a wall art sizing guide for Indian rooms: cover standard Indian room dimensions for urban apartments, what size to choose for a feature wall, how to create a gallery wall, and the specific sizes available in Chumbak’s wall art range. Include a simple size recommendation table. Add FAQPage schema with 5 sizing questions. This is a low-competition query type where Chumbak can establish a strong citation position quickly.
Gifting with Indian lifestyle accessories: ChatGPT housewarming and festive gift guide without Chumbak
Indian gifting occasions (Diwali, housewarmings, weddings, birthdays) are a significant buyer journey for lifestyle accessories. In tested queries for best Indian housewarming gifts 2026 and Diwali home decor gifts India, AI engines cited generic gifting listicles and marketplace category pages rather than Chumbak as a branded destination. Chumbak’s product range is highly suited to Indian gifting occasions but the brand does not have structured gifting content that AI engines can extract.
What to do: Publish an annual Indian gifting guide from Chumbak: segment by occasion (Diwali, housewarming, weddings, birthdays) and budget range (under Rs 500, Rs 500 to Rs 1500, above Rs 1500). Include specific product recommendations with brief explanations of why each product suits that occasion. Add FAQPage schema with 6 gifting questions. Update this page each year before Diwali to maintain freshness. This makes Chumbak the cited brand for Indian festive gifting in AI search.

All 6 Factor Scores

Full breakdown
Entity Clarity72/100
Chumbak has a strong and clearly established entity as India’s original quirky lifestyle brand. 15 years of press coverage in Economic Times, YourStory, Inc42, and lifestyle publications create a robust entity signal for AI engines. The brand is well-identified for general lifestyle and home decor queries.
Fix: The entity is strong but can be made more specific for product-category queries. Add structured Organization schema that clearly maps Chumbak to its product categories: wall art, home decor, lifestyle accessories, fashion, and kitchenware. This helps AI engines cite Chumbak for category-specific queries rather than just brand-recognition queries.
Extractable Structure60/100
The Chumbak website has more content than a pure product catalog but educational content specifically designed to answer buyer questions is limited. Category pages have product listings with minimal structured explanations. Blog content exists but may not be consistently structured with direct-answer openings and FAQPage schema.
Fix: Review the top 10 category pages and add a structured introduction to each: what this product category is, who it is for, what distinguishes Chumbak’s version, and what buyer questions it answers. Ensure blog posts open with a direct answer to the implied question before any preamble. This improves AI extractability of existing content without requiring new content creation.
Schema Markup52/100
Basic schema may be present through Chumbak’s e-commerce platform, but FAQPage schema is not consistently detected on category or content pages in tested queries. The gap between Chumbak’s content volume and its schema implementation means that structured content may not be recognised as a citable answer resource by AI engines.
Fix: Add FAQPage schema to all category pages that contain question-and-answer content. Add Article schema to blog posts. Add Organization schema to the homepage with complete entity description. These three additions will materially improve the rate at which AI engines recognise and extract Chumbak’s existing content as citation-ready.
FAQ Coverage48/100
Buyer questions specific to Chumbak’s product categories (quirky decor styling, gifting advice, sizing, care instructions, Indian design context) are not consistently answered in a structured format on the site. Some FAQ content may exist on product pages but it is not schema-backed or systematically published.
Fix: Identify the top 5 buyer questions per major product category (wall art, home decor accessories, kitchenware, lifestyle gifts) using search analytics and AI engine query testing. Publish dedicated answer pages for each question set. This creates a structured FAQ content layer that AI engines can cite for the high-volume buyer decision queries in Chumbak’s categories.
Answer-Led Content55/100
Some Chumbak content opens with lifestyle description and brand narrative rather than direct answers to buyer questions. Content that describes the Chumbak design philosophy without immediately answering what a buyer is trying to find out is less likely to be extracted and cited by AI engines.
Fix: Audit the top 20 content pages and revise opening paragraphs to lead with the direct answer to the page’s primary buyer question. For a quirky home decor category page: These are Chumbak’s quirky home decor pieces for Indian homes. They cover wall art, cushion covers, storage, and kitchenware in bold, India-inspired prints and are priced from Rs 299. This type of opening is extractable where a brand narrative opening is not.
Specificity & Evidence62/100
Chumbak has more specificity than the category average: the brand’s design philosophy, Indian aesthetic roots, and product range are described with reasonable specificity on the website. However, specific evidence (number of customers, years in business, design process, material quality standards) is not consistently presented in a machine-readable format.
Fix: Add a specificity layer to key brand claims: years in business (since 2010), number of unique designs, materials used, manufacturing partners, and any sustainability or quality certifications. Present these as structured data on the About page and in Organization schema. Specific evidence improves both entity authority and the likelihood of being cited for brand evaluation queries.
