GA4 & AI Overviews

Questions about GA4 traffic drops and Google AI Overviews

Why impressions stay flat while sessions fall, what to do about it, and how to measure brand value when AI intercepts the click.

Why did my GA4 traffic drop after Google AI Overviews expanded?

When Google AI Overviews appear for a query, users read the synthesized answer directly in search results without clicking through. Your page continues to rank and earn impressions, but the click no longer happens. This is the zero-click effect. It affects informational and how-to queries most severely. Rankings have not changed and there is no technical penalty. The correct response is to shift the success metric for informational content from sessions to AI citation rate.

My Google Search Console impressions are up but GA4 sessions are down. What does this mean?

This is the signature of the AI Overviews zero-click effect. Google shows your page in search results and counts an impression each time. But an AI Overview appears above and answers the query directly, so the user does not click. You are being seen but not visited. This is not a ranking problem or a technical issue. It is a structural shift in how users interact with informational queries in Google Search.

Will my informational content traffic ever recover after AI Overviews?

For query categories where AI Overviews now appear consistently, CTR-based traffic is unlikely to return to pre-AI Overviews levels. The zero-click dynamic is structural, not a temporary spike. The correct strategic response is not to wait for CTR to recover but to measure informational content by citation rate instead. If your content is cited inside the AI Overview, your brand is still reaching the buyer during the research phase, even without a click.

What queries are most affected by the Google AI Overviews traffic drop?

Informational queries with a clear single answer are most affected: how-to queries, definition queries, comparison queries, and process explanation queries. Transactional queries where the user clearly wants to visit a specific site or make a purchase are less affected. If your content answers common educational questions in your category, those pages are the most exposed to zero-click behavior.

How do I know if my content is being cited in a Google AI Overview?

Google Search Console does not yet have a dedicated AI Overviews citation report. The best method is to manually search the queries showing the impression-session divergence pattern in your data and check whether an AI Overview appears and whether your site is listed among the cited sources. Some GEO tools also track AI Overview citation rates for defined query sets automatically. See: the full GA4 traffic drop guide for a step-by-step diagnosis workflow.

Should I stop creating informational content since AI Overviews intercept the traffic?

No. Informational content is now a citation authority asset rather than a primary traffic driver for the query types where AI Overviews appear. Getting cited inside AI Overviews earns brand impressions with high-intent buyers during the research phase of a purchase, even when they do not click. Those buyers then return directly, search your brand, or engage through other channels. Brands that stop creating informational content because session metrics declined abandon the citation channel at exactly the wrong time.

What is the right KPI for informational content in the AI Overviews era?

Track impression count in Search Console for reach, AI Overview citation rate for the key queries in your category, and branded search volume as a downstream signal of AI-driven awareness. Sessions from informational content will structurally decline as AI Overviews expand across more query types. Building strategy around session recovery for these queries leads to abandoning high-value citation assets at the worst time.

The right replacement KPI is AI Share of Voice: how often your brand appears in AI-generated responses across your category's key queries. Read how to set up this measurement in GA4.
