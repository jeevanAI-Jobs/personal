30 Terms Defined

AI Visibility Glossary

Clear definitions for every term in AI visibility, generative engine optimization, and brand tracking across AI platforms.

Core AI Visibility Metrics

AI Share of Voice The fraction of all brand mentions in your category that belong to your brand in AI-generated responses. AI Visibility Score A composite 0-100 metric combining mention rate, recommendation rate, and citation rate across AI platforms. AI Mention Rate The fraction of AI queries in which your brand name appears in any context in the response. AI Recommendation When an AI system actively suggests your brand to a user, as opposed to merely mentioning it in passing. Citation Rate The fraction of AI responses in which your content is cited as a named source. Citation Authority The strength of a brand's content as a source that AI systems retrieve and cite in responses.

Foundational Concepts

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) The practice of structuring content so AI systems can extract and cite it as direct answers to user queries. Generative Search AI-synthesized responses that answer queries directly rather than providing a ranked list of links. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) An AI architecture that retrieves relevant documents at query time before generating a response. Conversational Search Querying AI systems using natural language questions and multi-turn dialogue rather than keyword strings. Vector Search Retrieval based on semantic similarity of meaning rather than keyword matching.

Entity & Brand Signals

Brand Entity A structured digital representation of a brand that AI systems can identify, describe, and reason about. Entity Authority The strength and breadth of a brand's recognition as a defined entity across AI training sources. Entity Disambiguation The process of correctly identifying which specific entity a name refers to when multiple entities share similar names. Brand Corroboration Consistent, accurate brand information across multiple independent authoritative sources. Source Corroboration How AI systems validate claims by finding agreement across multiple independent sources. Brand Information Accuracy The degree to which AI systems describe a brand correctly across category, product, customer, and geography.

GEO Practices

Structured Data Machine-readable markup (typically JSON-LD) that helps AI systems understand entities and content. Knowledge Graph An entity-relationship database used by AI systems to store and query structured knowledge about the world. llms.txt A proposed file format that allows websites to provide AI-optimized content summaries. GEO Content Brief A content planning document that specifies AI visibility requirements alongside traditional SEO goals. Prompt Optimization for Brands Structuring content and entity signals so buyers' AI queries produce responses that include the brand.

AI Search Surfaces

Google AI Overviews Gemini-powered summaries that appear above organic search results for relevant queries. Zero-Click AI Search AI-generated responses that fully answer a query without the user needing to click through to any website. AI Search Visibility A brand's overall presence in AI-generated search responses across platforms. Multi-LLM Visibility Consistent brand presence across multiple AI platforms simultaneously.

Tracking & Benchmarking

AI Brand Tracking Systematic monitoring of a brand's presence across AI platforms over time. AI Visibility Benchmarking Measuring a brand's AI presence against competitors and historical baselines. AI Cold Start The disadvantage new or unknown brands face in AI systems due to insufficient training data and entity signals. AI Dark Traffic Website visitors referred by AI systems that appear as direct traffic in analytics, with no visible referral source. Citation Decay The gradual decline in how often an AI system cites a brand as its content ages or is superseded.
