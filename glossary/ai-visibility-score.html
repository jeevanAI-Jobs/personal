What Is an AI Visibility Score?

A clear definition of the AI Visibility Score, what it measures, how different tools calculate it, and what score ranges suggest about a brand's AI presence.

Definition

An AI Visibility Score is a composite metric that aggregates a brand's presence, citation frequency, and recommendation rate across a set of AI-generated responses, expressed on a single normalized scale (commonly 0 to 100) to enable comparison over time and against competitors.

AI Visibility Score in Plain Terms

AI systems do not return ranked lists the way search engines do, so brands cannot simply check "position 1" to understand their presence in AI responses. An AI Visibility Score translates the messier reality of AI outputs into a single number: how often, how prominently, and in what context does this brand appear when AI systems answer questions in its category?

Different tools define and weight the inputs differently. Some weight citation rate (how often your content is sourced) most heavily. Others weight recommendation rate (how often AI explicitly recommends your brand). What the number represents depends on the tool. The common value is having a single comparable number that moves in response to the actions you take.

What Goes Into an AI Visibility Score

While formulas vary, most AI Visibility Scores draw on some combination of:

  • Mention rate: The fraction of queries in which the brand is named in any context.
  • Recommendation rate: The fraction of queries in which the brand is actively recommended or placed in a shortlist.
  • Citation rate: The fraction of queries in which the brand's content or website is cited as a source.
  • Platform breadth: Whether the brand appears across multiple AI platforms or only one.
  • Sentiment or framing: Some tools note whether the brand is mentioned positively, neutrally, or in a cautionary context.

Why Track an AI Visibility Score

Without a summary metric, AI visibility data tends to be a flood of individual responses that is hard to act on. A score compresses that data into a number that is easy to report to stakeholders, easy to track over a quarter, and easy to compare against a competitor. It signals whether actions taken (publishing new content, earning new reviews, building new citations) are moving the needle.

As a lagging indicator, it confirms whether a strategy is working. As a competitive benchmark, it shows the distance between your brand and the category leader. For a deeper look at what the scores suggest across categories, see the AI visibility score benchmarks guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI Visibility Score?

It is a composite metric, typically on a 0 to 100 scale, that aggregates how often a brand appears, is cited, and is recommended in AI-generated responses across platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

What is a good AI Visibility Score?

A score above 60 out of 100 generally suggests the brand appears in most relevant queries on at least one platform. The most useful reference point is your category's top competitor rather than an absolute benchmark.

How often does an AI Visibility Score change?

Scores can shift after any significant change in the information environment: a new product review, a press mention, or a burst of content production. Some brands run weekly scans to track short-term movement; others run monthly to track trends.

Does a high AI Visibility Score mean AI is sending traffic?

Not necessarily. A high score means the brand appears in AI responses. Whether those appearances translate to clicks or conversions depends on the context: whether the response links to your site, whether the user is in a buying mode, and which AI platform was used. AI visibility and AI-attributed traffic are related but distinct.
