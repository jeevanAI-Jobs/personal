What Is Structured Data?

A clear definition of structured data and schema markup, the most important types for AI visibility, and how to implement them correctly.

Definition

Structured data is machine-readable markup added to web pages, typically using JSON-LD and Schema.org vocabulary, that explicitly describes the type, properties, and relationships of the page's content to help AI systems, search engines, and knowledge systems parse and use that information accurately.

Structured Data in Plain Terms

AI systems and search engines understand web pages by reading text, but text is ambiguous. A paragraph that mentions "the company was founded in Bengaluru in 2024" tells a human reader clearly what it means, but a machine parsing the page cannot be certain whether "the company" refers to the page owner, a company being reviewed, or a company being discussed in an article.

Structured data resolves that ambiguity. It says, in machine-readable terms: "This page is about the Organization named 'Jeevan AI'. It was founded in 2024. Its headquarters is in Bengaluru. Its official website is jeevanai.co.in." The machine no longer needs to infer; the information is explicit.

Key Structured Data Types for AI Visibility

  • Organization: Describes your brand entity. Include name, description, url, logo, foundingDate, sameAs links (Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase). Add this to your homepage.
  • FAQPage: Marks up question-and-answer pairs. Makes individual answers extractable for AI answer surfaces and featured snippets. Use on any page with a Q&A section.
  • Article: Adds datePublished, dateModified, and author (as Organization, not Person) to blog posts and guides. Helps AI systems assess content freshness and authority.
  • Product: Describes a product's name, description, brand, and review summary. Helps AI systems accurately describe what you offer.
  • DefinedTerm: Marks up glossary definitions, associating a term with its definition and its containing term set.
  • BreadcrumbList: Describes the page's position in the site hierarchy. Helps AI systems understand how content is organized.

Why Structured Data Matters in 2026

As AI systems increasingly power the first layer of brand discovery, the difference between a brand being described accurately and being described inaccurately or omitted can have real commercial consequences. Structured data is the most direct technical signal a brand can send to help AI systems get it right. It is also one of the few signals a brand controls entirely: unlike third-party citations or press coverage, schema markup can be added to any page without external dependencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is structured data?

Structured data is machine-readable markup, typically JSON-LD using Schema.org vocabulary, added to web pages to explicitly describe their content type, properties, and relationships for AI systems and search engines.

What is the most important structured data type for AI visibility?

Organization schema on your homepage is the most foundational, as it defines your brand entity. FAQPage schema on content pages is the most immediately impactful for AI answer surfaces.

How do I add structured data to my website?

The simplest approach is to add a JSON-LD script block in the head of the relevant pages. You can use Jeevan AI's free schema markup generator to create the correct JSON-LD for your use case.

Does structured data guarantee better AI visibility?

It does not guarantee better visibility, but it reduces the chance of inaccurate entity representation and makes your content more easily parseable by AI systems. It is a foundation, not a complete strategy.
