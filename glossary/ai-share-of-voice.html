What Is AI Share of Voice?

A clear definition of AI Share of Voice, how it differs from traditional Share of Voice, and how brands measure it across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

Definition

AI Share of Voice is the percentage of AI-generated responses, across a defined set of queries, in which a brand is mentioned or recommended, expressed relative to the total mentions across all competing brands in that category.

AI Share of Voice in Plain Terms

When a buyer asks ChatGPT to recommend the best project management tool for a remote team, the platforms it names and the order in which it names them represent Share of Voice in that moment. AI Share of Voice aggregates those moments across many queries and many buyers to produce a percentage: out of all the times AI recommended someone in your category, how often was it you?

It is a brand metric, not a keyword metric. The unit is the brand mention or recommendation, not a ranking position or click. For brands selling to buyers who now start product research in AI systems rather than search engines, it may serve as an early-stage funnel metric worth tracking alongside impressions and organic traffic.

How AI Share of Voice Is Measured

The measurement process involves three steps. First, define a query set that represents the kinds of questions real buyers ask when researching your category. Second, run those queries across the AI platforms you want to track (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot). Third, parse the responses to identify every brand mentioned, then calculate each brand's share of total mentions.

  • Query set design: Include both generic category queries ("best CRM for startups") and comparison queries ("CRM alternatives to Salesforce"). The mix shapes what the metric captures.
  • Platform scope: AI Share of Voice varies significantly across platforms. A brand may have strong presence in Perplexity but low presence in ChatGPT. Measuring one platform gives a partial picture.
  • Response variability: AI systems may return different answers on different runs of the same query. Averaging across multiple runs produces a more stable estimate.

Why AI Share of Voice Matters

Research on buyer behavior in 2025 and 2026 suggests that a growing share of B2B and D2C buyers use AI systems at the top of the funnel, to shortlist vendors before visiting any website. In that context, a brand's AI Share of Voice may affect whether it appears on the shortlist at all. Brands that track this metric tend to use it alongside traditional brand awareness metrics and AI citation rate.

It also serves as a competitive intelligence signal. Shifts in a competitor's AI Share of Voice may indicate changes in their content strategy, press coverage, or review site presence before those changes are visible in search rankings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Share of Voice?

AI Share of Voice measures how often your brand appears in AI-generated responses compared to competitors, expressed as a percentage of total brand mentions across a tracked set of queries on platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

How is AI Share of Voice calculated?

Count your brand's mentions in AI responses across a query set, divide by total mentions of all brands in the category, and express as a percentage. Tools like Jeevan AI automate this across multiple platforms and query sets.

What is a good AI Share of Voice score?

There is no universal benchmark because it depends on category competitiveness and the number of brands in the query set. Tracking your share over time and comparing it to known competitors is more useful than comparing to an absolute number. The AI visibility benchmarks guide covers typical ranges by category.

How do I improve my AI Share of Voice?

Improving AI Share of Voice typically involves building entity authority through consistent brand signals across authoritative sources, earning citations on review sites and in industry publications, and creating content that AI systems tend to draw on for your category queries.
