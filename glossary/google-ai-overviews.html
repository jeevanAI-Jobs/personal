What Are Google AI Overviews?

A clear definition of Google AI Overviews, how they differ from featured snippets, which sources they tend to draw on, and what brands can do to improve their appearance in them.

Definition

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated response summaries that Google displays above traditional search results for a significant and growing share of queries, synthesizing information from multiple sources into a single response using Google's Gemini AI model, with expandable source citations.

Google AI Overviews in Plain Terms

Before AI Overviews, Google's search results page opened with ranked links. Now, for a growing share of queries, it opens with a generated summary that synthesizes the answer before the links appear. The generated summary is powered by Gemini and cites multiple sources. Users can expand the sources section to see which pages were used.

For brands, this creates a new visibility surface above traditional rankings: appearing in the AI Overview means the brand's content is influencing the first response the user sees. Not appearing means the user sees the generated summary, which may reference and recommend competing brands, before ever reaching the traditional results.

How to Improve Appearance in Google AI Overviews

Observed patterns in which sources tend to appear in AI Overviews:

  • Strong traditional rankings: Pages that rank on page 1 for a query appear more frequently in the corresponding AI Overview. SEO fundamentals (backlinks, content quality, E-E-A-T signals) remain a prerequisite.
  • Direct answer structure: Content with clear, direct answers to the specific query question, ideally in the opening paragraph of a section, is more likely to be cited.
  • FAQPage and HowTo schema: Structured data markup makes content easier for Google to parse and cite accurately.
  • Content freshness: Google's AI Overviews tend to cite recently updated content for time-sensitive queries. Keep key pages updated with dates.
  • E-E-A-T signals: Google's existing quality signals (expertise, experience, authoritativeness, trustworthiness) carry over into AI Overview source selection.

For a detailed guide, see how to get cited in Google AI Mode.

Why Google AI Overviews Are a Priority Brand Visibility Surface

Google handles the largest volume of search queries globally. AI Overviews appear at the top of the page for those queries, above the results that traditional SEO competes for. For brands selling to buyers who research via Google, AI Overviews are the first content layer those buyers encounter. Building a presence in that layer is increasingly important alongside, and sometimes in place of, traditional ranking optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries, powered by Google's Gemini model, that appear above traditional search results for many queries. They synthesize information from multiple sources and cite those sources in an expandable section.

Do Google AI Overviews replace featured snippets?

They are separate features that can co-exist. Featured snippets extract a single passage from one page. AI Overviews synthesize a response from multiple sources. Both appear above traditional organic results, but AI Overviews are longer and more comprehensive.

Can I opt out of appearing in Google AI Overviews?

You can use robots meta tags to prevent Google from indexing specific pages, which would prevent them from being cited. However, opting out of AI Overviews specifically is not currently possible through a dedicated mechanism separate from standard crawl directives.

Does strong traditional SEO help with AI Overviews?

Yes. Pages with strong traditional search rankings appear more frequently in AI Overviews for related queries. SEO fundamentals remain a necessary input, though they are not sufficient on their own: content structure and answer quality also matter.
