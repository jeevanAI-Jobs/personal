Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Short version: We collect only what is needed to run your AI visibility scans and show you results. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

1. Who We Are

Jeevan AI is an AI brand visibility platform operated under Indian law. Our platform is accessible at jeevanai.co.in and the dashboard at dashboard.jeevanai.co.in.

For questions about this policy or your data, reach us through the dashboard or the contact form on our website.

2. What Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used to create and identify your account)
  • Name (optional, used for display purposes)
  • Payment information (processed by Razorpay; we do not store card numbers or bank details)

Brand and Scan Data

  • Brand names, website URLs, and competitor names you configure in your account
  • Tracked queries you define for AI visibility testing
  • Scan results: AI-generated responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews as recorded at the time of each scan
  • Visibility scores, citation data, and factor analysis derived from scans

Usage Data

  • Pages visited within the dashboard
  • Actions taken (scans launched, reports viewed)
  • Device type, browser, and approximate location (country level) via standard server logs

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not access or store the content of your website beyond what you explicitly provide
  • We do not read your email, documents, or files
  • We do not purchase or import data about you from third parties

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the service: Run AI visibility scans on your behalf, store results, and show you reports and trend data
  • To manage your account: Send plan confirmation emails, usage alerts, and billing receipts
  • To improve the platform: Understand aggregate usage patterns to fix bugs and improve features (never at an individual level)
  • To comply with law: Retain records as required under Indian tax and financial regulations

We do not use your brand data or scan results to train AI models. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Data Sharing

We share data with the following categories of service providers only to the extent needed to operate the platform:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Database, authentication, and cloud infrastructure. Data is stored in Google Cloud data centres. Google Cloud Privacy
  • Razorpay: Payment processing for Indian customers. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • AI platforms (OpenAI, Perplexity, Google, Anthropic): We send query prompts to these platforms as part of running visibility scans. These prompts include your tracked queries and may reference your brand name. We do not send your personal account information.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and periodic security reviews.

No method of data storage or transmission is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your account, we will notify you as required under applicable law.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and brand data within 30 days. Scan result data may be retained in anonymised, aggregated form for platform analytics.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years as required under Indian GST regulations.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you by contacting us through the dashboard
  • Correct inaccurate data via your account settings or by contacting us
  • Delete your account and associated data from the Settings page in your dashboard
  • Export your scan history and tracked query data (contact us to request an export)
  • Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent, without affecting prior processing

We will respond to data requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our marketing website (jeevanai.co.in) uses only essential cookies for site functionality. The dashboard uses session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect dashboard functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Jeevan AI is a business tool intended for use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
