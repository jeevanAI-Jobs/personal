The full page. Not just the brief.

Every AI visibility gap Jeevan AI finds comes with a complete, publish-ready content draft grounded in your brand data. Schema markup and SEO meta included. No writer needed.

What it is

A content draft is the actual page, not the instructions for writing it

When Jeevan AI identifies an AI visibility gap, it does not stop at telling you what to write. It writes the page. The draft includes the full body copy, structured with H2 headings, answer-led paragraphs, and the specific evidence AI engines need to extract and cite your brand. You review it, paste it into your CMS, and publish.

Why this matters for AI visibility specifically

AI engines do not cite brands because they are well-known. They cite brands because a specific page on their site answers a specific buyer question better than any other page they found. Content briefs tell you to write that page. Jeevan AI content drafts are that page. The difference is whether you fix the gap this week or after finding a writer, briefing them, reviewing drafts, and editing revisions.

How it works

Every draft is built from three data sources specific to your brand

The draft is not a template with your brand name filled in. It is generated from a combination of your scan results, your Knowledge Base, and your social signal data.

Source 1

Your scan results

The draft targets the specific buying factor (Trust, Use Case Fit, Pricing, Ease of Use, or Quality) that is causing the gap, and the specific query type where your competitor is appearing instead of you.

Source 2

Your Knowledge Base

Your pricing, features, differentiators, case studies, and brand-specific claims are pulled from your Knowledge Base and woven into the draft so the content is accurate to your brand.

Source 3

Social signal data

Reddit threads and Google signals show what buyers actually ask about your category. The draft is structured to answer those real buyer questions, not hypothetical ones.

The Knowledge Base is what makes the draft yours

Every Jeevan AI brand has a Knowledge Base: a structured store of facts, numbers, pricing, feature descriptions, testimonials, and differentiators. When the content draft is generated, it draws from this store so the page contains your real claims, not generic statements. The more complete your Knowledge Base, the more specific and immediately publishable each draft will be.
